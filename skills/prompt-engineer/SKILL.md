## CapCut Editing Notes
Ratio: 9:16 for TikTok/Reels | 1:1 for Instagram feed | 16:9 for YouTube
Hook overlay text: Bold, white, drop shadow, first 3 seconds
Captions: Auto-caption ON, clean font, white with black outline
Music: Trending audio from TikTok sound library (check weekly)
Transitions: Cut on beat, minimal effects, let food speak

## Opus Clip Settings (long to short)
Target length: 45-90 seconds for TikTok/Reels
Hook detection: ON
Auto-caption: ON
Virality score: Only export clips scoring 80+
