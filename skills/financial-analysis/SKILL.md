# Skill: financial-analysis
## When to use
Use when Mothy shares P&L screenshots, performance reports, revenue numbers, food cost %, payroll %, or asks about financial performance comparison.

## How to Read a Hotel P&L (SOP Performance Snap)
Columns: Actual | Forecast | Budget | Last Year | A vs B | A vs LY
- **A vs B** = Actual vs Budget (red = under, green = over)
- **A vs LY** = Actual vs Last Year
- **GOP** = Gross Operating Profit (revenue minus all dept costs)
- **NOP** = Net Operating Profit (after undistributed expenses)

## Key Metrics to Always Pull
1. Total Revenue (Actual vs Budget %)
2. Occupancy % (Actual vs Budget)
3. F&B Revenue + Payroll %
4. GOP % of Revenue
5. Department-by-department variance

## Payroll % Analysis Rule
- High payroll % ≠ overspending on labour
- Could mean revenue underperformed against which roster was built
- Always check: is the $ spend over or under budget? Only then conclude

## Food Cost Benchmarks (Accor / Hotel context)
- Target food cost: 28-32%
- Beverage cost: 18-25%
- F&B payroll target: 38-42%
- F&B profit margin: 28-35%
- GOP target: 30-40% of total revenue

## KitchenFounder Benchmarks (from guide P&Ls)
- HK Cha Chaan Teng: 49% net margin (highest)
- Argentine Asado: $2.94M revenue (highest)
- Hawaiian Poke Bar: 41.5% net margin
- Spanish Tapas: 40% net margin
- Singaporean Hawker: 34% net margin

## Comparison Framework
When comparing two properties (e.g. Novotel vs Pullman):
1. Revenue volume (bigger = more resilient)
2. Payroll % (context: which got offsite/windfall revenue?)
3. Occupancy miss (both down = precinct problem, not individual)
4. Outlet-by-outlet (BB's vs Bacar)
5. What saved/hurt each property

- Payroll $ under budget → % high due to revenue miss
- Pullman payroll 50% vs Novotel 49% → not isolated problem
- Same Olympic Park precinct → shared headwinds
- Food cost recovery 40%→34% in one week = fast response when present
