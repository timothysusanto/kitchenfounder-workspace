## KitchenFounder Content Strategy

## TikTok Hook Formulas (first 3 seconds = everything)
Pattern 1 — Shocking stat: "Most restaurants fail in year one. Here is why yours wont."
Pattern 2 — Hot take: "Your food cost calculator is lying to you."
Pattern 3 — Story open: "I walked into a failing restaurant in Bangkok. Here is what I fixed first."
Pattern 4 — Direct address: "If you want to open a restaurant, watch this first."
Pattern 5 — Curiosity gap: "The one number that determines if your restaurant survives."

## Instagram Content Pillars (rotate these)
1. Education — recipe costing, break-even, staff ratios, kitchen design
2. Behind the scenes — real kitchen moments, prep, service, plating
3. Concept spotlight — feature one guide cuisine per post
4. Myth-busting — "You do not need a huge budget to open a restaurant"
5. Social proof — when reviews/sales come in, share them

## Caption Formula
Line 1: Hook (same energy as TikTok hook)
Lines 2-4: Value — 3 bullet points or a short story
Line 5: CTA — "Link in bio" or "Comment GUIDE and I will send it"
Hashtags: 5-8 max, mix broad + niche
Best hashtags: #restaurantowner #foodbusiness #restaurantlife #kitchenfounder #fbindustry #cheflife #restauranttips #foodentrepreneur
