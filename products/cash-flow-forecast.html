💡 KitchenFounder Survival Rules

Rule 1 — The 6-Month Rule: You need enough capital to cover 6 months of fixed costs BEFORE revenue stabilises. Most restaurants fail in months 2–4, not because the food is bad, but because they ran out of cash during the ramp-up.

Rule 2 — True Labour Cost: Never budget base wages only. Add FICA (7.65%) + SUTA + Workers Comp + Health. True cost is typically 18–25% higher than base wages. Use the Labour Planner for exact numbers.

Rule 3 — The 30% Food Cost Ceiling: If your blended food cost exceeds 30%, your margins compress fast. Monitor weekly. A 2% food cost blowout on $100K monthly revenue = $2,000 straight off your profit.

Rule 4 — Delivery Fees Kill Margins: A 25% delivery platform fee on a $20 dish means you net $15. After food cost (30%) = $6. After labour (32%) = nothing. Build delivery pricing separately from dine-in.

Rule 5 — Cash ≠ Profit: You can be profitable on paper and still run out of cash. Track your bank balance weekly. Set a minimum cash floor (e.g. $20K) — if you go below it, cut costs immediately.
