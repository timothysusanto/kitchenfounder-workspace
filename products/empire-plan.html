Chapter Four · IP Registered May 30, 2026
HospitalityOS
The 6-Module System
The world's first AI-native end-to-end hospitality operating system. One platform. One click. Every module connected. IP owned by Timothy Susanto — documented and timestamped May 30, 2026.

AI observes. AI processes. AI prepares the action. The operator taps Confirm or Edit. Never manual data entry. Never more than one meaningful action per task.

— The One-Click Principle · HospitalityOS Core Philosophy
01 · SupplySnap

Camera AI reads supplier price boards in under 3 seconds. Pushes to PurchasePlus automatically. Market benchmarking. Alerts when prices are 10% above market.

02 · RosterIQ

Bookings → AI forecasts covers → suggests roster → calculates true on-cost labour %. Integrates with Zambion, Deputy, Humanforce.

03 · ProfitPulse

Live P&L fed by POS, SupplySnap, and RosterIQ. Weekly report auto-generated every Monday. AI variance analysis in plain English.

04 · TableFlow

TikTok native "Book Now" inside videos. Instagram DM AI booking. LINE integration for Thailand (52M users). Bangkok-first launch.

05 · ImmerseDine

Meta Quest 3 and Ray-Ban Meta Glass. WebAR — no app needed. 3D food visualisation at real scale. World's first social-to-AR-to-booking funnel.

06 · ChefBrain

Natural language daily brief. Voice command mode during service. Cross-module anomaly detection. Seasonal demand forecasting. WhatsApp and LINE delivery.

Origin Story

Mothy observed PurchasePlus (Accor's procurement platform) showing stale supplier prices versus the real invoice prices coming in. The gap between what the system thought things cost and what they actually cost was costing operators thousands every month. That observation became SupplySnap. SupplySnap became HospitalityOS.

Competitive Moat

Insider operational knowledge (10+ years). Network effect (suppliers + venues, two-sided marketplace). Unique AI dataset after 12 months. Social-native first-mover (no OpenTable on TikTok). AR preview 18–24 month technical lead.

IP Documentation 07
Chapter Five · IP Registered May 20, 2026
KitchenFounder
AI Kitchen System
The next frontier — physical AI integrated into the restaurant kitchen and the home. Four products. Two markets. One platform. IP owned by Timothy Susanto — documented and timestamped May 20, 2026.

Product 1 — Fridge AI

Restaurant · Commercial

A camera and AI system mounted inside commercial refrigerators that tracks inventory in real time. As items are used, the system automatically updates stock levels, generates purchase orders, and alerts when critical items are running low. Integrates with PurchasePlus and SupplySnap for automatic reordering at best market price.

  • Computer vision identifies items and quantities via camera
  • Auto-generates purchase orders when below par level
  • Tracks FIFO compliance — alerts when older stock is being passed over
  • Temperature anomaly detection — HACCP compliance built in

Product 2 — Pass Monitor

Restaurant · Service

An AI camera system mounted at the pass (the counter where kitchen sends food to service) that monitors every plate leaving the kitchen. Computer vision checks portion size, presentation consistency, and temperature compliance before the plate reaches the guest.

  • Portion size verification — flags over or under portioning
  • Presentation consistency — compares against reference image
  • Thermal imaging — confirms hot food is hot at point of service
  • Speed tracking — alerts when tickets are sitting too long at the pass

Product 3 — Floor Coach Robot

Restaurant · FOH Automation

A mobile AI robot that moves through the restaurant floor during service. It monitors table status, assists with food delivery on simple runs, and provides real-time feedback to the service team via earpiece on upsell opportunities and table timing.

  • Table status tracking — who has ordered, who is waiting, who is ready to pay
  • Food delivery assistance — simple plate runs, freeing staff for service
  • Upsell coaching — whispers to waiter "table 8 ready for dessert recommendation"
  • Guest sentiment detection via facial recognition and body language AI

Product 4 — Home Consumer Version

Consumer · B2C Market

A scaled-down home kitchen AI assistant — countertop device with camera and voice. Helps home cooks with recipe guidance, portion coaching, inventory management, and meal planning. The consumer on-ramp that builds brand awareness at household level and drives data back to the commercial platform.

  • Recipe guidance with real-time visual coaching
  • Pantry inventory — knows what is in the fridge and what to cook
  • Meal planning and automatic grocery list generation
  • KitchenFounder recipe library integration — 300+ guides as content
