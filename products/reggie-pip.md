PURPOSE OF THIS PLAN

This Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) has been developed to provide Regie Soriano with a clear, structured framework for improving her performance and conduct to the standard expected of a Demi Chef at Novotel Ibis Sydney Olympic Park.

This plan is not a disciplinary action. It is a formal support tool designed to give Regie every opportunity to meet the required standards. Failure to meet the targets outlined in this plan within the review period may result in further disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

This plan has been developed independently of any complaint process and is based solely on documented performance observations.

SECTION 1 — AREAS OF CONCERN

1.1 TIMESHEET AND ATTENDANCE COMPLIANCE

Concern:
A repeated pattern of timesheet irregularities has been observed and documented, including:
- Clocking in earlier than the rostered start time without supervisor knowledge or approval
- Taking meal breaks at the end of the shift rather than at the designated mid-shift break time
- Clocking out earlier than the end of the rostered shift without supervisor knowledge or approval

This pattern was previously documented during Regie's time at Pullman (with Chef Marvin and Emma) and has continued at Novotel. Timesheet irregularities constitute a serious conduct matter as they may result in incorrect payment of wages and misrepresentation of hours worked.

Required Standard:
- Clock in at the exact rostered start time or notify the supervising chef immediately if arriving early or late
- Take all meal breaks at the designated time as directed by the supervising chef
- Clock out at the exact rostered finish time or notify the supervising chef before leaving
- All variations to rostered hours must be approved in advance by the supervising sous chef or head chef

Measurement:
- Zero unexplained timesheet discrepancies during the 8-week review period
- Supervisor sign-off on timesheet compliance to be recorded weekly

---

1.2 TASK COMPLETION AND TIME MANAGEMENT

Concern:
Consistent feedback from supervising sous chefs indicates that Regie frequently leaves allocated tasks incomplete at the end of her shift. This includes:
- Daily Duty Prep (DDP) not completed to the required standard
- Canapé preparation not completed within allocated shift time
- Cleaning duties partially completed or left for the next shift
- Requiring repeated direction on standard daily tasks that should be completed independently at Demi Chef level

A Demi Chef is expected to manage their own time effectively within their 7.5-hour shift, prioritise tasks without constant supervision, and complete all assigned duties before clocking out or formally hand over incomplete tasks to the next chef with a clear written or verbal handover.

Required Standard:
- All DDP tasks completed or formally handed over with written note by end of shift
- Canapé and service preparation completed within the time allocated on the prep sheet
- Cleaning duties completed as per the cleaning schedule before end of shift
- No task to be left incomplete without written handover to supervising chef

Measurement:
- Daily task completion to be assessed by supervising sous chef
- Written handover log to be maintained — Regie to sign and submit each shift
- Any incomplete tasks to be documented by the supervising chef with reason noted

---

1.3 COMMUNICATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Concern:
On multiple occasions Regie has provided accounts of incidents that have been inconsistent with accounts provided by other team members. Specifically:

- The beef cheek incident of May 14–15, 2026: Regie's account to management was directly contradicted by Chef Jovi's account. This created a situation where management could not rely on the information provided.

Clear, honest, and accurate communication with supervisors is a fundamental requirement of any kitchen role. When questions are raised about missing items or incomplete tasks, the expected response is a clear, factual account — not deflection or redirection to other team members.

Required Standard:
- All communication with supervisors to be accurate, direct, and factual
- If Regie is unsure of an outcome or did not witness something, the correct response is "I don't know" — not an account that attributes responsibility to others without certainty
- Any concerns about workload, task completion, or team dynamics to be raised directly with the supervising chef at the time — not withheld and raised later

Measurement:
- No further incidents of inconsistent accounts during the review period
- Regie to actively raise operational concerns in real time with the supervising chef

---

1.4 CONDUCT — KITCHEN PROPERTY AND PERSONAL BELONGINGS

Concern:
On approximately May 12, 2026, Regie was observed placing a box of disposable gloves (hotel property) into her personal bag. Chef Sekda (JJ) witnessed this and reported it to the supervising chef. Kitchen supplies are hotel property and may not be removed from the premises without authorisation.

Additionally, Regie has been reminded on multiple occasions that personal bags are not permitted in kitchen areas for food safety and workplace security reasons. Personal belongings must be stored in the designated personal locker at all times during the shift.

Required Standard:
- No hotel property (including but not limited to food items, packaging, gloves, utensils, or cleaning supplies) to be removed from the premises without written authorisation from the Head Chef
- Personal bags and belongings to be stored in the personal locker before commencing the shift — not brought into kitchen areas
- Full compliance with the hotel's property and security policies at all times

Measurement:
- Zero incidents of unauthorised removal of hotel property during the review period
- Zero instances of personal bags in kitchen areas during the review period
- Compliance to be monitored by supervising chefs
