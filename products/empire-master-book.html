"3 things every restaurant owner gets wrong on their menu." Teach something real and specific. End with: "Full system in the guide — link in bio." Education converts to sales better than any ad.

The Decision Framework — When to Spend More

Spend more on a channel ONLY when:

  • ROI is proven ≥ 3× (spend $1, earn $3+)
  • You have at least 2 weeks of data
  • The increase is capped at 2× current spend (no doubling overnight)

Never spend on:

  • Any tool before you have revenue to justify it
  • Branding/design before product is selling
  • Ads on cold traffic before your organic content is proven
  • Any platform you haven't personally tested for 2 weeks first

The Revenue Reinvestment Rule:

Take 20% of every month's net business revenue and put it into the next month's marketing budget. The business funds its own growth. Your salary stays your savings engine. Never mix them.
