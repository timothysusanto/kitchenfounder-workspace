6-Month License Timeline
A typical path from decision to license in hand. Timelines vary by state.
Month 1
  • Determine which license type you need for your concept
  • Hire a liquor license attorney in your state
  • Begin gathering all required documents
  • Research local zoning restrictions and distance rules
Month 2
  • Submit state application with all supporting documents
  • Submit local/city application simultaneously
  • Attend Community Board meeting if required (NY)
  • Post public notice if required by your state
Month 3
  • Background check processing for all principals
  • Public protest/notice period (30 days in CA)
  • Respond promptly to any objections filed
  • Follow up with attorney on application status
Month 4
  • Follow up with state licensing authority
  • Local/city approval expected this month
  • Prepare staff training materials (responsible service)
  • Arrange surety bond if required by your state
Month 5
  • State approval expected — confirm with attorney
  • Receive temporary permit if available in your state
  • Begin ordering alcohol inventory under temp permit
  • Train all staff on responsible service laws
Month 6
  • Full license received — display prominently at premises
  • Complete all required server certification (BASSET/RBS/TABC)
  • Set up compliance calendar for renewal dates
  • Open your bar — you made it!
📋 Documents Checklist
Gather these before filing — missing documents cause costly delays.
📄Signed lease or proof of ownership
📐Floor plan (to scale, showing bar area)
🏢Business entity documents (LLC / articles)
🔢EIN confirmation letter
👤Background check consent forms (all owners)
💰Personal financial statements (owners >10%)
🍽️Menu showing food service
📸Photos of premises (interior + exterior)
🏗️Certificate of occupancy (or applied for)
🔐Surety bond (some states required)
