Station Presentation Setup

Station 3 — The Grazing Board

  • Surface: Long timber boards or slate slabs · minimum 2× 120cm boards or one continuous 2.4m run
  • Build method: Cheeses first (anchor points) · meats folded between · crackers fanned · condiments in small white bowls · crudités in tall glasses at ends · fruit clusters in gaps · bruschetta on a separate small board
  • Height variation: Use small risers, overturned ramekins under boards to create levels
  • Labels: Small chalkboard picks for each cheese variety and allergen flags
  • Colour rule: Build dark to light left to right — meats (dark) → cheese (medium) → crackers/veg (light)
  • Maintenance: Check every 30 min. Refill crackers first (they go fastest). Meats every 45 min.
  • Chef: Stand at side — slice cheese to order, assist guests, keep board looking full and clean
Visual tip: This station sets the tone for the whole room. Set it up last so it looks perfect when doors open. Take a photo at 6:55pm — shows well in post-event reporting to Joe/client.

Quantity Rationale — How Numbers Were Calculated

Cocktail event rule: guests average 8–12 pieces/bites over 3 hours. 500 pax × 10 avg bites = 5,000 total serves. Distributed unevenly — Station 2 (Pasta) and Station 5 (Sliders) take ~35% each. Station 1 (Tacos) ~25%. Stations 3 + 4 supplement and circulate. All quantities calculated at 110% (10% buffer) for wastage, spillage, and late arrivals.
