🚀 KitchenFounder Opening Week Run Sheet

Soft Launch · Media Night · Hard Open · Daily Ops

FREE TOOL
🥂 Soft Launch — Friends & Family Night

Run 2 seatings max. Comp everything or 50% discount. Take notes on every table. This is your rehearsal — not your opening.

Day Before
📦 Full Prep Checklist
  • All mise en place complete and labelled
  • FOH fully set — tables, linens, cutlery, glassware
  • POS system tested — all menu items entered and priced
  • Reservation list confirmed and printed
  • Kitchen walkthrough — fridge temps, labelling, FIFO
  • Staff briefing — roles, covers, expectations
  • All menu items costed and plated — photo each one
  • Emergency contacts list printed and posted
Day Of — 8:00 AM
Owner Morning Tasks
  • Walk every inch of the restaurant — owner eyes only
  • Confirm all staff confirmed for tonight
  • Final supplier delivery check — nothing missing
  • Review reservation list — any VIPs or allergies flagged
  • Check bathrooms, lighting, music, temperature
Day Of — 10:00 AM
Kitchen Final Prep
  • Head Chef confirms all dishes ready to execute
  • All sauces, stocks, components prepped
  • Ticket printer tested
  • Cleaning schedule confirmed for during service
  • All dietary/allergen dishes identified and flagged
Day Of — 4:00 PM
FOH Pre-Service Setup
  • Team arrives — full uniform check
  • Floor walkthrough with manager
  • Service sequence briefing — greet, order, food, dessert, bill
  • Allergy protocol reminder
  • Upsell targets set for tonight
During Service
Owner On The Floor
  • Visit every table — introduce yourself
  • Ask: "What could we do better?"
  • Note every complaint or hesitation
  • Watch kitchen pass — timing issues?
  • Count every dish that comes back unfinished
After Service — Debrief
⚠️ Fix These Before Media Night
  • Score every dish 1–5 for taste, presentation, speed
  • Identify top 3 fixes needed
  • Any dishes to remove or modify?
  • Service timing issues to address
  • Staff performance notes — who needs extra coaching?
  • Write fixes list — assign owner for each item
📸 Media Night — Influencer & Press Preview

8–12 guests: local food bloggers, Instagram food accounts, journalists, community figures. This is the night that gets reviewed. Everything must be perfect.

2 Weeks Before
📨 Guest List & Invitations
  • Identify 15 targets — invite 15, expect 8–12
  • Instagram food accounts (5K+ local followers)
  • Local food journalists or bloggers
  • Community figures, business owners, influencers
  • Send personalised invite — not a mass email
  • Include: date, time, dress code, parking info, RSVP deadline
  • Brief them: "We'd love your honest feedback and any content you'd like to share"
Day Of — Non-Negotiables
Owner Standards for Tonight
  • Full menu served — no shortcuts
  • Paired beverages with each course
  • Head Chef comes out to every table to introduce dishes
  • Owner present all night — never in kitchen
  • No visible stress — even if things go wrong backstage
  • Every guest photographed (with permission) — tag on social same night
Kitchen Standards
Kitchen Media Night Brief
  • Every plate must be identical — no variation
  • Portions consistent and generous
  • Timing: 15 min between courses maximum
  • Head Chef to expedite personally
  • No dishes leaving pass below standard — ever
FOH Standards
FOH Service Protocol
  • Every team member briefed on who the guests are
  • Service: elegant, not rushed
  • Know every dish — ingredients, technique, sourcing story
  • Offer to describe each dish as it arrives
  • Check back 2 minutes after food arrives
  • Complimentary dessert wine or digestif at the end
Within 24 Hours After
⚠️ Follow-Up is Critical
  • Personal thank you message to every guest
  • Tag them in your social post (ask permission first)
  • Share any content they posted — add to stories
  • Request a review on Google if they enjoyed it
  • Note any feedback they gave — fix before hard open

Kitchen Head Chef Arrives

  • All deliveries received and checked

Owner Full Walkthrough
  • Walk every space — kitchen, FOH, bathrooms, storage
  • Health permit, liquor license displayed
  • Music levels right, lighting set, temperature comfortable
  • Signage and menus in place
  • POS fully loaded and tested
10:00 AM
FOH Team Briefing
  • Full team present — no late arrivals tolerated today
  • Review reservation list and special requests
  • Today's targets: covers, revenue, avg check
  • 3 upsell items identified for today
  • "This is the day people will remember. Be your best."
11:00 AM
Kitchen Final Prep Check
  • All stations clean and ready
  • Head Chef calls readiness from each station
  • First ticket arrives in 60 minutes — everyone ready
  • Portion sizes confirmed — consistent with training
12:00 PM — Doors Open
Owner On The Floor All Day
  • Greet every table personally
  • Watch kitchen pass — flag any timing issues early
  • Handle any complaint immediately — don't let it escalate
  • Count covers every hour
  • Check in with Head Chef every 30 minutes
After Service
⚠️ Day 1 Debrief (15 Minutes)
  • Actual covers vs target
  • Actual revenue vs target
  • Top 3 things that went well
  • Top 3 things to fix tomorrow
  • Any staff issues to address?
  • Any menu items that didn't sell?
  • Write it down — you'll forget by morning
