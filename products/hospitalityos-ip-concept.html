Executive Summary
The Problem With Hospitality Software Today

The global hospitality industry runs on fragmented, outdated, manual technology. A hotel's purchasing team uses one platform (PurchasePlus, FutureLog, Ivalua). Their HR team uses another (Zambion, Deputy, Humanforce). Their finance team uses a third (MYOB, Xero, SAP). Their marketing team posts on TikTok and Instagram manually. None of these systems talk to each other.

The result: a Head Chef in Sydney orders produce at stale catalogue prices, over-staffs on a slow Tuesday because the roster isn't connected to the booking system, gets surprised by a labour blowout because the rostering tool doesn't show true on-costs, and has no single view of whether this week is profitable.

HospitalityOS solves all of this. One AI-native platform. One click per action. Every system connected.

This is not an incremental improvement on existing tools. This is a ground-up reimagining of how hospitality operations run — built with AI at the core, not bolted on after.

📋

Stale Supplier Prices

Produce prices move daily. PurchasePlus catalogues are updated weekly at best. Invoice surprises are routine. A $1.50/kg error on a 50kg order = $75 gone before service starts.

Industry loss: $2.3B/year globally
👥

Roster-Reality Gap

Rosters built without cover forecasts. Labour cost shown as base wages only — true on-costs (FICA, super, workers comp) hidden. Budget blowouts discovered at month end, not shift start.

Avg overspend: 8-12% of labour budget
💰

No Real-Time P&L

Financial reports arrive 2-3 weeks after the period closes. By then the decisions are made. Operators fly blind every service, every week.

72% of operators lack live P&L visibility
🍽️

Booking Disconnected from Operations

OpenTable, TheFork, Google — bookings land in one silo. The kitchen gets a cover count print-out. There is zero automated connection between bookings, prep planning, and staffing.

20-30% over/under-prep waste per service
📱

Social Media ≠ Booking

Gen Z and millennials discover restaurants on TikTok and Instagram. But booking still requires leaving the app, finding the website, and using a third-party form. Conversion lost at every step.

67% booking intent abandonment rate

Zero Pre-Visit Experience

You book a restaurant based on 5 photos and 23 reviews. No spatial sense of the room. No idea if your table is next to the toilet. No way to see what the food actually looks like in 3D.

$0 technology investment in this space today
Core Design Philosophy
The One-Click Principle

Every feature in HospitalityOS is designed around a single rule: the end user should never need to do more than one meaningful action. AI handles everything else — reading, processing, routing, calculating, alerting.

Old technology asks the user to input data, then process it, then interpret it, then act on it. HospitalityOS inverts this. The AI observes, understands context, prepares the action, and presents a single decision point: Confirm or Edit.

Platform Architecture
Six Integrated AI Modules

HospitalityOS is built as six deeply integrated modules sharing a single data layer. Every module feeds every other module in real time. No exports. No manual re-entry. No silos.

📸 SupplySnap — AI Supply Chain Intelligence
Real-time supplier price updates via camera AI · Market benchmarking · Automated PO management
Camera AI reads supplier price boards, invoices, delivery dockets in <3 seconds
GPT-4 Vision + OCR extracts SKU, item name, unit, price automatically
Live market price benchmarking (Sydney Markets, USDA, Bangkok wholesale)

Technical Architecture
The Unified Data Layer

Every module in HospitalityOS shares a single operational data layer. This is the core architectural innovation — not just connecting APIs, but building a unified hospitality data model where supply, labour, financial, and guest data are all first-class citizens of the same schema.

When a booking arrives from TikTok, it immediately updates the cover forecast in RosterIQ, which adjusts the labour cost projection in ProfitPulse, which updates the cash flow forecast, which triggers a prep sheet update in SupplySnap. All of this happens in under 500 milliseconds, automatically, without any human action.

AI Stack: GPT-4 Vision (image reading) · Whisper (voice commands) · Custom fine-tuned model on hospitality data · Google Cloud Vision fallback · Embedding-based anomaly detection

Integration Layer: PurchasePlus API · Zambion/Deputy webhooks · Square/Toast/Lightspeed POS · Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks · TikTok Business API · Instagram Graph API · Meta Spatial SDK · LINE Messaging API

Competitive Advantage
Why This Can't Be Easily Copied

1. Insider Operational Knowledge. This platform was designed by a working Head Chef with 10+ years of F&B consulting experience across Accor properties. Every feature solves a real operational pain that existing software has failed to address. You can't replicate this insight from a boardroom.

2. The Network Effect. Every supplier who joins SupplySnap makes the platform more valuable for every connected venue. Every restaurant on TableFlow creates more social content that drives more diners to the platform. This is a two-sided marketplace with compounding defensibility.

3. The AI Data Moat. After 12 months of operation, HospitalityOS will have the world's most detailed real-time dataset of hospitality supply prices, cover patterns, labour efficiency ratios, and dining behaviour. This dataset becomes a proprietary asset that gets more valuable with every venue added — and impossible to replicate from scratch.

4. Social-Native First-Mover. No existing restaurant booking platform has native TikTok or Instagram booking. OpenTable is a 1998 paradigm with a 2024 coat of paint. We build for where the customer already is — their phone, their feed, their favourite app.

5. The AR Preview Barrier. Building food and restaurant AR at scale requires AI-generated 3D modelling, deep meta SDK integration, and extensive content operations. This is an 18–24 month technical lead that cannot be closed quickly by incumbents.
