Chef's Recommendation · Rationale + Full Summary05
Why We Recommend 3 Proteins at Dinner
Based on our experience hosting international sports teams at this property, we have observed a consistent pattern: elite athletes consume protein at approximately 2–3× the rate of leisure guests and deplete single-protein buffet stations well before service ends, creating a substandard experience for the final 20–30% of athletes to be served. Three proteins per dinner service solves this by distributing demand across options, ensuring every athlete has full access to a complete protein regardless of when they arrive. It also accommodates the dietary diversity within a squad — athletes managing weight, athletes in heavy recovery, athletes with personal preferences — without requiring special requests.
