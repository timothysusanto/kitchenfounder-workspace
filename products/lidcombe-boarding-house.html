📋 Company Structure — How It Works
Step 1: Register a Pty Ltd company (e.g. "Lidcombe Property Management Pty Ltd") — ~$500 via ASIC
Step 2: Company owns/operates the boarding house (or enters a management agreement)
Step 3: You are employed by the company as "Property Manager" at market salary
Step 4: Company pays 25% tax (small business rate) on remaining profit
Step 5: Retained profit can be reinvested, distributed as dividends (franked), or used to acquire more property

Key deductions you can claim: Your salary, mortgage interest, all operating costs, depreciation on fit-out (Div 40 & 43), repairs, insurance, accounting fees, vehicle (if used for property management), home office component

⚠️ Get an accountant: Company setup and property tax is specialist territory. Budget ~$3,500–$5,000/year for a good property accountant. It pays for itself.

⚠️ Risks & How to Mitigate
Top Risks
🔴 DA rejected — zoning doesn't permit boarding house
🔴 Interest rate spike — 8%+ crushes cash flow
🔴 High vacancy — problem tenants or bad location
🟡 Renovation blowout — always add 20% contingency
🟡 Management burden — 20 people = 20 problems
🟡 Legislative change — NSW boarding house rules evolving
🟢 Currency risk — if you move to Bangkok, AUD fluctuates
Mitigations
✅ Town planner due diligence BEFORE purchase
✅ Fixed-rate portion on mortgage (hedge rate risk)
✅ Strong tenant screening process from day 1
✅ 20% reno contingency built into budget
✅ Hire a live-in site manager (reduce rent for 1 room)
✅ Property lawyer reviews all agreements annually
✅ Company structure = AUD income stays in company
