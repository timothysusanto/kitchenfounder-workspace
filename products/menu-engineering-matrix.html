⭐ Stars — Keep & Promote

These items are your profit engines. Protect their food cost, feature them prominently on the menu, and train staff to recommend them.

🐄 Plowhorses — Increase Profitability

Popular but thin margins. Consider a small price increase, reduce portion size slightly, or substitute a lower-cost ingredient.

❓ Puzzles — Drive Volume

Great margins but low volume. Reposition on the menu, create combos, or have staff recommend them to boost popularity.

🐕 Dogs — Review or Remove

Low margin and low volume. Consider removing, repricing, or rebranding these items. Every menu slot has an opportunity cost.
