Silver Service — Sequence & Protocol 02
Service Sequence
Silver service means the food travels from kitchen to guest on a platter — the service team serves individually at the table. Every movement deliberate. Every portion presented. The sequence below is the order of the four proteins, from lightest to the crown.
First
Chicken Ballotine
Light · Delicate · Opens the palate
Second
Lamb Rack
Medium · Bold · Builds intensity
Third
Arroz Negro
Seafood · Complex · Contrast
Last · Crown
A9 Wagyu Sirloin
The finale · Nothing after it
Silver Service Rules — This Lunch
✦ Two service team members per platter — one holds, one serves
✦ Service always from the left of the guest
✦ Sauce poured or spooned from the right
✦ Ladies first, then clockwise around the table
✦ All 12 covers plated within 90 seconds of first guest
✦ Platter returns to pass — never left on the table
✦ Sides remain on the table as sharing plates throughout
✦ No verbal description unless guest asks — let the food speak
Platter Setup — Kitchen to Table
✦ All proteins portioned and plated on silver oval platter
✦ Platter garnished at the pass before leaving kitchen
✦ Sauce travels separately in a warm silver jug or sauceboat
✦ Service team receives platter at pass, walks in together
✦ One person at each end of the table — work toward centre
✦ Warm plates set before platter arrives — always
✦ Head Chef visible at pass for wagyu course — intentional
Why This Sequence
Chicken first — the most delicate protein. Clean flavours. The porcini cream and truffle open the palate without overwhelming it. Owners settle in.
Lamb second — persillade crust and sauce paloise build intensity. The white bean purée anchors the course. Conversation is flowing by now.
Arroz negro third — the contrast course. Seafood, ink, saffron. Different texture, different temperature, different colour. It resets the palate before the finale.
Wagyu last — always last. Nothing competes with A9 marble score. The Head Chef at the pass for this course is intentional. It signals: this matters. The crown course deserves the crown chef.

Course 01 of 04 — First Protein · Lightest 03
First
Course 01 · French Technique · Chicken
Chicken Ballotine — Porcini Cream
Truffle mousse stuffing · Sauce suprême · Asparagus · Truffle oil
12 Portions · Silver Platter Service
Ingredients — 12 Portions
Free-range chicken breasts, large 12 pcs
— Truffle Mousse —
Chicken thigh meat (cold) 500g
Thickened cream (very cold) 200ml
Egg whites 2
Black truffle paste 50g
Flat parsley, fresh thyme as needed
— Porcini Cream —
Dried porcini mushrooms 80g
Fresh Swiss brown mushrooms 400g
Shallots, garlic, thyme 3 / 2 cloves / as needed
Dry white wine 200ml
Thickened cream 500ml
Chicken stock 300ml
Cold unsalted butter (finish) 80g
— Sauce Suprême —
Reduced chicken stock 600ml
Cream, shallots, white wine 200ml / 3 / 100ml
Cold butter (mount) 120g
— Garnish —
Pencil asparagus spears 36 spears
Truffle oil, fleur de sel to finish
Method — Day Before: Mousse & Ballotines
1.Blitz cold thigh meat, egg white, salt in food processor. Drizzle in cold cream slowly — stop the moment it's combined. Fold in truffle paste and herbs by hand. Keep ice-cold throughout.
2.Butterfly each breast open. Lay flat, spread mousse evenly, roll tight in clingfilm. Twist ends. Refrigerate overnight — shape sets perfectly.
3.Day of: Poach ballotines at exactly 72°C for 18 minutes. Remove, unwrap, sear in foaming butter until golden all over. Rest 3 min. Slice on bias — 2 rounds per person showing truffle cross-section.
Porcini Cream — 1hr Before Service
1.Rehydrate dried porcini in 300ml warm water 25 min. Strain carefully — reserve liquid.
2.Sauté shallots and garlic in butter until soft. Add fresh mushrooms — cook until deeply golden, 8 min. Add rehydrated porcini.
3.Deglaze with wine, add strained porcini liquid + stock + cream. Reduce by one-third until coating consistency. Mount with cold butter. Season deeply.
Sauce Suprême — Fresh Day Of
1.Sweat shallots, deglaze wine, reduce. Add stock and cream, simmer to nappe. Mount with cold butter — ivory, velvety, glossy.
Asparagus — To Order
1.Blanch 90 sec in boiling salted water. Refresh in ice. Finish in foaming butter to order — lemon squeeze, fleur de sel.
🍽️ Platter Setup — Kitchen Pass

Arrange 12 sets of 2 ballotine rounds on the oval silver platter, each set slightly overlapping to show the truffle cross-section. Spoon porcini cream beside each set — do not pour over, let the guest see the cut face. Three asparagus per person laid alongside. Garnish the platter with small thyme sprigs at each end. Keep warm under cloche. Sauce suprême travels in a separate warm silver sauceboat.

🤍 Silver Service Instructions — Course 01
Step 1Warm plates set before platter leaves kitchen. Two service staff carry platter and sauceboat together. Step 2Enter from the right side of the room. First server begins at the host's right, works clockwise. Second server mirrors from opposite end of table. Step 3Use serving spoon and fork to place 2 ballotine rounds and asparagus on warm plate. Porcini cream spooned alongside from the platter. Step 4Third team member follows with sauceboat — asks "sauce, Mr/Ms [name]?" Pours from the right. One generous spoon — not flooding the plate. Step 5Once all 12 served, one server adds 3 drops of truffle oil to each plate tableside using a small dispenser. This is the finishing gesture for this course.
All 12 guests served within 75 seconds of first guest. Platter returns to kitchen. No cloche drama on this course — the truffle cross-section is the reveal.

Course 02 of 04 — Second Protein · Builds Intensity 04
Second
Course 02 · French Technique · Lamb
Lamb Rack — Persillade Crust
White bean & truffle purée · Sauce paloise · Ratatouille niçoise · Tapenade
12 Portions · 3 Cutlets Per Person · Silver Platter Service
Ingredients — 12 Portions
Lamb racks (French trimmed) 4 full racks (8-bone)
Dijon mustard 100g
— Persillade Crust —
Sourdough breadcrumbs, fresh 300g
Flat parsley (very finely chopped) 2 bunches
Garlic, minced 4 cloves
Lemon zest, EVOO 2 lemons / 60ml
— White Bean & Truffle Purée —
Cannellini beans, soaked overnight 700g dry
Garlic, bay, thyme (cooking) as needed
Quality EVOO 160ml
White truffle oil (finish) 15ml
Lemon juice, salt, white pepper to season
— Sauce Paloise (Mint Béarnaise) —
Egg yolks 8
Clarified butter, warm 500g
Fresh mint (not tarragon) 2 bunches
White wine vinegar, shallots, peppercorns reduction
— Ratatouille · Tapenade —
Zucchini, eggplant, capsicum, tomato 350g each
Kalamata olive tapenade 120g
Method
1.Bean purée (day before): Cook soaked beans with garlic, bay, thyme until very tender. Drain, reserve liquid. Blitz with EVOO, truffle oil, lemon — add cooking liquid for silky consistency. Pass through sieve. Keep warm in bain-marie.
2.Ratatouille: Dice all vegetables 1cm uniform. Cook each type separately in EVOO with garlic until just tender. Combine, season, add torn basil. Room temp is fine — reheat gently to serve.
3.Persillade: Combine parsley, garlic, breadcrumbs, lemon zest, EVOO — vivid green, holds together when pressed.
4.Lamb (service day): Season racks, sear hard on all sides in cast iron — 3–4 min total. Brush with Dijon. Press persillade firmly onto meat side.
5.Roast at 200°C — 9–10 min. Internal temp 57°C for perfect pink. Rest 6 min minimum under foil. Portion into 3-cutlet servings between the bones — clean decisive strokes.
6.Sauce paloise (day of, hold warm): Reduction of vinegar, shallots, peppercorns. Whisk yolks over bain-marie to ribbon. Drizzle in warm clarified butter very slowly. Finish with fresh mint chiffonade. Hold at 55°C max.
🍽️ Platter Setup — Kitchen Pass

On the silver platter: small swoosh of white bean purée for each set, ratatouille mound next to it. Three lamb cutlets placed standing upright against the ratatouille, bones pointing up — architectural and striking. Small tapenade quenelle at the base of each set. Garnish platter ends with fresh thyme and lemon zest curls. Sauce paloise travels in a warm silver sauceboat — never on the platter itself.

🤍 Silver Service Instructions — Course 02
Step 1Clear Course 01 plates simultaneously — two staff, right side, no reaching across guests. Step 2Fresh warm plates set. Allow 3 minutes between courses — not rushed, not too long. Step 3Platter enters the room — two staff. The standing lamb cutlets are immediately visible. Let the room react before serving begins. Step 4Use spoon and fork: place bean purée swoosh first, ratatouille beside it, then stand the three cutlets upright. Tapenade last. Step 5Sauce paloise poured from the right in a controlled arc alongside — not over — the ratatouille. Ask first: "sauce paloise?" Some guests may decline. Step 6Fleur de sel finish on the persillade crust tableside — small pinch from a small bowl. This is the final touch. It makes the crust sing.
The standing cutlets will draw attention when the platter arrives. Pause two seconds at the entrance. Let owners see it. Then serve.

Course 03 of 04 — Third Protein · Contrast & Drama 05
Third
Course 03 · Spanish Soul · Seafood
Saffron Arroz Negro
King prawn bisque · Bomba rice · Two king prawns per person · Saffron alioli
12 Portions · Served in Warm Pasta Bowl · Silver Service
Ingredients — 12 Portions
Bomba rice 720g
Squid ink sachets 5 sachets (20g)
— Prawn Bisque (Day Before) —
Prawn shells + heads 1.5kg
White onion, garlic, tomato paste 2 / 6 cloves / 80g
Pimentón de la Vera (smoked paprika) 20g
Brandy or dry sherry 100ml
White wine, water 300ml / 2L
— Sofrito —
White onion, finely diced 2 large
Garlic cloves 6
Roma tomatoes, grated 6 pcs
Pimentón, EVOO 15g / 80ml
White wine (for rice) 200ml
— King Prawns —
King prawns U8, shell-on 24 pcs (2 per guest)
Smoked paprika, EVOO, garlic as needed
— Saffron Alioli —
Egg yolks 6
EVOO 400ml
Garlic, lemon juice 3 cloves / 2 lemons
Saffron (bloomed in 30ml warm water) 1.5g
Prawn Bisque — Day Before
1.Roast shells and heads in EVOO at high heat until deep red — press with spoon to extract maximum flavour. Don't rush this step.
2.Add onion, garlic, tomato paste, pimentón — cook 5 min. Deglaze with brandy/sherry, flambé if comfortable. Add wine then water.
3.Simmer 30 min. Pass through fine sieve, pressing hard. Reduce to 2.2L intense orange-red bisque. Season. Refrigerate overnight — the flavour deepens.
Arroz Negro — Service Day (in batches)
1.Sofrito: cook onion very slowly in EVOO 20 min — must be sweet and melted. Add garlic, grated tomato, pimentón. Cook further 10 min until paste-like.
2.Add bomba rice, toast 2 min. Add squid ink + white wine — stir until absorbed. Then add hot bisque ladle by ladle without stirring.
3.Medium heat, 18 min. Allow socarrat to form — listen for the crackle at the base. This is not optional. Rest 3 min covered.
King Prawns — Last 3 Minutes Before Service
1.Peel leaving tail on. Butterfly the back, devein completely. Season with paprika, EVOO.
2.Smoking hot dry pan — 60 seconds each side only. Char marks. No overcooking. Time this precisely against the platter assembly.
Saffron Alioli — Make Morning Of
1.Bloom saffron in warm water 10 min — deep gold. Hand whisk yolks with garlic and lemon. Drizzle EVOO very slowly to emulsify. Fold in saffron water. Season.
🍽️ Platter Setup — Warm Pasta Bowls

12 warm pasta bowls pre-set on the platter in two rows of six. Spoon arroz negro into each bowl — build gentle height, not flat. Two king prawns placed crossed on top, tails pointing up and out. Generous quenelle of saffron alioli to one side — gold against jet black. Three drops smoked EVOO around the edge. Flat parsley leaf and trimmed lemon quarter. The visual contrast of black rice and gold alioli is the presentation — the platter arriving at the table will stop the conversation.

🤍 Silver Service Instructions — Course 03
Step 1This course is served in pre-filled warm pasta bowls — not plated at the table. The platter carries the bowls to the table, staff places each bowl in front of each guest. Step 2One staff member per side of the table — each carries a tray of 6 bowls on a folded service cloth. Place from the right of each guest. Step 3Extra saffron alioli travels in a small bowl at the centre — placed on the table for guests who want more. This is the one communal element of this course. Step 4Warm crusty bread offered from a separate basket as this course arrives — the bisque at the bottom of the bowl is for soaking. Let guests know.
The jet-black rice arriving in a pasta bowl is the most visually dramatic course. Time the entry — wait until all conversation has naturally paused, then enter. Let the colour do the work.

Course 04 · The Crown · A9 Wagyu
A9 Wagyu Sirloin — Chef Carved
Sauce bordelaise · Wasabi cream · Chimichurri verde · Head Chef at the pass
12 Portions · Whole Roast · Silver Platter · Head Chef Present
Ingredients — 12 Portions
A9 Wagyu sirloin, whole (2.5–3kg) 1 whole piece
Coarse sea salt, cracked black pepper generous — only seasoning
EVOO (sear only) 30ml
— Sauce Bordelaise —
Bordeaux / Shiraz wine 750ml
Beef demi-glace (good quality) 500ml
Shallots, thyme, bay leaf 6 / as needed
Bone marrow rounds, poached 12 rounds
Cold unsalted butter (mount) 100g
— Wasabi Cream —
Crème fraîche 200g
Fresh wasabi paste (or quality tube) 25g
Lemon juice, fleur de sel to season
— Chimichurri Verde —
Flat parsley, garlic 2 bunches / 4 cloves
EVOO, sherry vinegar 150ml / 40ml
Dried chilli flakes, fleur de sel to taste
Sauces — Day Before or Morning Of
1.Bordelaise: Sweat shallots, add wine — reduce by ¾. Add demi-glace, thyme, bay — simmer 20 min. Strain. Bring to service temp, mount with cold butter and bone marrow rounds last minute. Deep, glossy, wine-forward.
2.Wasabi cream: Fold wasabi into crème fraîche, season with lemon and fleur de sel. Make fresh morning of — flavour sharpens over time. Refrigerate.
3.Chimichurri: Blitz parsley and garlic roughly — keep texture. Add EVOO, sherry vinegar, chilli. Season. Should be bright green and punchy. Make day of.
A9 Wagyu Sirloin — The Centrepiece
1.Season generously with coarse salt and cracked pepper only. No marinade. No herbs. The marble is the flavour — respect it.
2.Bring to room temperature 1.5 hours before cooking. This is not negotiable for a piece this size.
3.Sear in very hot cast iron pan with minimal EVOO — hard crust on all sides, 4–5 min total. Don't move it.
4.Roast at 180°C. Internal temperature target: 52°C for rare-medium rare. A wagyu A9 needs no more — the fat is liquid at 52°C. That is the experience.
5.Rest minimum 20 minutes under foil, in a warm area. Do not skip. The carryover will bring it to 55°C.
6.Head Chef carves at the pass — 4–5mm slices against the grain. The marble should glisten. Each slice fanned slightly on the platter. No hiding in the kitchen for this course.
🍽️ Platter Setup — The Crown Presentation

Large oval silver platter, warmed. Wagyu slices fanned across the platter in a single elegant row — 2–3 slices per person, showing the marble score clearly. Bone marrow rounds placed beside every second portion. The three sauces travel in separate silver sauceboats — bordelaise, wasabi cream, chimichurri — carried by three dedicated service staff, one per sauce. This course has the most staff on the floor simultaneously. It should feel like an event.

🤍 Silver Service Instructions — Course 04 · The Crown
Step 1Head Chef Timothy visible at the pass as this platter is assembled. Owners can see him. This is the gesture — the chef shows himself for the most important course. Step 2Three staff enter together: platter carrier + two sauceboat carriers. Enter in single file. The room will notice. Step 3Platter carrier serves wagyu slices and bone marrow from the left. 2–3 slices per person — use spoon and fork, lay flat and slightly overlapping on the warm plate. Step 4First sauceboat: bordelaise. "Bordelaise sauce, Mr/Ms [name]?" Pour from right in a controlled arc. This is the classical choice. Step 5Second sauceboat: wasabi cream. Offered to each guest — some will want both. Allow it. Never rush a guest on this course. Step 6Third sauceboat: chimichurri. Placed on the table as a shared condiment — not individually poured. Guests help themselves throughout the course. Step 7Fleur de sel in a small pinch bowl placed on the table. This is the only course where the guest seasons themselves — wagyu deserves that autonomy.
Do not rush this course. It is the last protein. Owners should sit with it. Three staff, three sauces, one platter — this is the most choreographed moment of the lunch. Rehearse it once before service.

Sharing Sides & Dessert Programme 07
Sharing Sides — Set on the Table
Four sharing plates set on the table before Course 01 arrives and remain throughout all four protein courses. Guests serve themselves. Replenished quietly without interruption.

Gratin Dauphinois

Individual cast iron dishes · Gruyère · Cream

Désirée potatoes 3mm, layered with cream, garlic, thyme, gruyère. Baked 90 min covered, 20 min uncovered until golden and set. Each guest has their own small cast iron dish — placed in front of them when sides are set. Rich, comforting, classically French.

↑ Individual cast irons signal care and attention from the first moment.

Pommes Dauphine

Choux potato puffs · Chive crème fraîche

Duchess potato folded through choux pastry, piped into ovals, fried until shatteringly crisp and airy inside. Served in a warm silver bowl with a small pot of chive crème fraîche at the centre. Nobody expects this. Everyone remembers it.

↑ Replenish every course — they disappear fast.

Haricots Verts — Beurre Noisette

Fine green beans · Hazelnut butter · Lemon

Blanched fine green beans finished in foaming beurre noisette, lemon juice, fleur de sel. Served in a warm oval dish. The nuttiness of the butter elevates a simple vegetable to something memorable. Pairs perfectly with all four proteins.

NUT FREE — beurre noisette only, no added nuts.

Heirloom Tomato & Burrata

Whole burrata broken tableside · Aged balsamic · Basil

A cascade of heirloom tomatoes in red, yellow, and green. Whole burrata placed at the centre — broken open by the service captain before Course 01 arrives. Aged balsamic, Ligurian EVOO, fleur de sel, micro basil. A visual statement that anchors the table from the start.

↑ The burrata breaking is the first visual moment of the lunch. Time it well.
Dessert — Three Acts
Cleared after Course 04. Brief palate cleanser pause. Then the sweet finale in three acts.

🍫 Valrhona Chocolate Fondant

Individual · Baked to order · Liquid centre

70% Valrhona bittersweet chocolate fondant baked to order — 10 min, 190°C. Centre must flow when cut. Crème anglaise poured tableside from a warm jug. Cocoa dusted. This is the individual plated dessert served silver service — same protocol as the mains.

↑ Bake-to-order shows real confidence. The flowing centre is the moment.

🧀 Artisan Cheese & Honeycomb

Shared board · Centre table · After fondant

Brillat-Savarin (triple cream) · Comté 18-month · Roquefort · Aged cheddar. Raw Australian honeycomb as centrepiece. Fig jam · quince paste · muscatels · sourdough crackers · sliced pear and apple. Set at table as a shared centrepiece after the fondant plates are cleared.

↑ Honeycomb in its frame on the board = owners take a photo.

✨ Mignardises — Passed Tray

Four petit fours · Tiered tray · One bite each

Salted caramel chocolate truffle (Valrhona ganache) · Citrus pâte de fruit (jewel-bright, sugar-dusted) · Warm financier (brown butter, one bite) · Candied orange peel in dark chocolate. Passed on a tiered silver tray — the visual arrival of the tray is its own moment.

↑ Passed simultaneously with coffee service.

☕ Coffee & Tea Service

Individual order · Salted truffle on saucer

Espresso · Long black · Flat white — made to order. English Breakfast · Earl Grey · Green tea · Peppermint · Chamomile. Milk · Oat milk · Sugar. Each cup arrives with one salted caramel truffle on the saucer. This detail costs almost nothing. It is what owners mention when they describe this lunch to others.

↑ One truffle on the saucer. The detail they remember.

Head Chef's Final Word

Silver service is not just a style — it is a declaration that this guest matters enough to be served personally. Every movement the team makes in that room says something about this kitchen, this hotel, and this Head Chef. Rehearse the wagyu course entry once before service. Walk it through. The owners will feel the difference between a team that practiced and one that didn't.

Four courses. Four proteins. One sequence. Cook with your whole soul — they will feel it.
