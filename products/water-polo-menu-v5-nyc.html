What NYC-Level
Event Catering Looks Like
Great Performances, Constellation Culinary, Abigail Kirsch, and Pinch Food Design service the US Open, the Met Gala, and major international sports events from Madison Square Garden to Yankee Stadium. This is the standard we are benchmarking against.

The 5 Principles of NYC-Level Event Catering

1. Stations, Not Buffets. Food is served from interactive stations with a chef present. Guests choose. Chefs plate. The energy is theatrical, not institutional.

2. Global Flavour Without Apology. NYC caterers do not water down cuisine for a broad audience. They trust that food-literate guests — which athletes at a World Cup are — can handle real flavour.

3. One Signature Moment Per Service. Every meal has one dish or presentation that is unexpected. Not gimmicky — genuinely considered. Something that earns a photograph.

4. Visual Architecture. Everything is plated or displayed with intention. No chafing dish lids left open. No beige trays. Every station looks like it was styled for editorial photography.

5. Athlete Intelligence. NYC sports caterers understand high-performance nutrition is non-negotiable. Clean protein, complex carbs, anti-inflammatory ingredients, minimal ultra-processed food. Flavour and function coexist.

Reference: Great Performances NYC · Constellation Culinary · Pinch Food Design · Abigail Kirsch · Haute Cuisine Group

Dietary Standards
Every Athlete Eats Well

Gluten Free

  • All soups GF by default
  • GF pasta option every lunch
  • GF flatbread at every bread station
  • GF dessert option every service
  • All grain bowls GF by default

Halal

  • All proteins Halal-certified
  • Pork completely absent from all menus
  • Alcohol removed from all cooking
  • Halal certification available on request
  • Separate serving utensils throughout

Vegan / Vegetarian

  • Minimum 2 VG protein options per service
  • VG stations clearly labelled
  • No cross-contamination with meat stations
  • VG desserts every service
  • Plant-based milk at beverage station

100% Nut Free

  • Zero nuts in any dish across all 16 services
  • Pumpkin seeds replace pine nuts
  • Sunflower seeds replace almonds
  • All suppliers confirmed NF-facility
  • NF signage on all stations
