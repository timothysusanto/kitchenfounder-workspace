📰 KitchenFounder Tips
Food Cost Rule: If your blended food cost exceeds 32% two weeks in a row — it's not bad luck. It's a system problem. Check your top 5 items in the Food Cost Calculator.
Operations Insight
Labour Truth: Most operators budget base wages. Your true labour cost is 18–25% higher when you add FICA, workers comp, and health insurance. Use the Labour Planner to see the real number.
Finance Insight
Menu Engineering: The average restaurant menu has 3–4 "Dogs" costing the kitchen time and money. Run the Menu Matrix monthly. Kill one Dog per quarter.
Menu Insight
Cash Survival: The 6-Month Rule — you need cash to cover 6 months of fixed costs before revenue stabilises. Most restaurants fail in months 2–4, not because the food is bad, but because they ran out of cash.
