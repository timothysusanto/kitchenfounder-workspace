🦐 Seafood · Spanish Influence · 4 Portions
Saffron Arroz Negro
King prawn bisque · Bomba rice · Two king prawns · Saffron alioli · Served in a warm pasta bowl
Ingredients — 4 Portions
Bomba rice 240g
Squid ink sachets 2 sachets (8g)
King prawns (U8, shell-on) 8 pcs
Prawn shells (bisque base) 500g
White onion, finely diced 1 large
Garlic cloves, minced 4 cloves
Roma tomatoes, grated 3 pcs
White wine, dry 150ml
Prawn bisque (see below) 700ml
Saffron threads 0.5g
EVOO 60ml
Smoked paprika (pimentón) 5g
Flat parsley, lemon garnish
— Saffron Alioli —
Egg yolks 2
EVOO 150ml
Garlic (1 clove), lemon juice as needed
Saffron (bloomed in 10ml warm water) pinch
Prawn Bisque — Make Day Before
1.Roast prawn shells in EVOO until deep red and fragrant — high heat, don't rush
2.Add onion, garlic, tomato paste, pimentón — cook 5 min
3.Deglaze with brandy (if available) then white wine — reduce
4.Cover with water, simmer 30 min, pass through fine sieve — press hard
5.Reduce bisque to intense, sweet, deep orange stock — approx 700ml. Season well.
Arroz Negro — Service Day
1.Sofrito: low-slow cook onion in EVOO 20 min until melted and sweet. Add garlic, grated tomato, pimentón — cook further 10 min
2.Add bomba rice, toast in sofrito 2 min — each grain coated
3.Add squid ink + white wine — stir until absorbed
4.Add hot prawn bisque ladle by ladle — no stirring after first addition. Medium heat.
5.Cook 16–18 min. Allow socarrat (crust) to form on base — listen for the crackle
6.Rest off heat 3 min covered
King Prawns — To Order
1.Peel prawns leaving tail on. Butterfly the back, devein
2.Season with salt, smoked paprika, EVOO
3.Sear in smoking hot dry pan — 60 seconds each side only. Char marks essential.
Saffron Alioli
1.Bloom saffron in warm water 10 min — deep gold colour
2.Hand whisk egg yolks with garlic and lemon — drizzle EVOO very slowly to emulsify
3.Fold in saffron water — golden, thick, fragrant
🍽️ Plating — Warm Pasta Bowl

Warm a deep wide pasta bowl — heat is essential for this dish. Spoon arroz negro into the centre, building height slightly. Two king prawns placed on top, tails up and crossed elegantly — one facing each direction. A generous quenelle of saffron alioli to the right side of the bowl, gold against the jet black rice. Three drops of smoked EVOO around the edge. Flat parsley leaf and a quarter lemon wedge, skin trimmed clean. The bowl does the work — let the contrast of black rice and gold alioli be the presentation.

🍋 Condiments & Table Accompaniments
Saffron alioli — served in the bowl
Lemon wedge — trimmed, no pith
Extra alioli on side if requested
Smoked EVOO drops — tableside finish
Pimentón de la Vera — pinch at table
Warm crusty bread — absorb the bisque
Chef's Note

The socarrat — the caramelised rice crust on the bottom — is what separates arroz negro from every other rice dish. When owners scoop to the bottom and hear the scrape, they know. Make the bisque the day before. The depth of flavour you get from properly roasted prawn shells overnight is what makes this dish taste like it came from a restaurant in Barcelona.

4 Portions Bisque: Day before Service time: 12 min GF · DF Seafood option

🥩 Lamb · French Technique · 4 Portions
Lamb Rack — Persillade Crust
White bean & rosemary purée · Sauce paloise · Ratatouille niçoise · Tapenade
Ingredients — 4 Portions
Lamb rack (3 cutlets per person) 2 full racks
Dijon mustard 40g
— Persillade Crust —
Sourdough breadcrumbs, fresh 120g
Flat parsley, finely chopped 1 bunch
Garlic, minced 2 cloves
Lemon zest, EVOO 1 lemon / 30ml
— White Bean Purée (replaces potato) —
Cannellini beans, soaked overnight 300g dry
Garlic (2 cloves), bay leaf, thyme as needed
Good quality EVOO 80ml
Lemon juice, white truffle oil to taste
— Sauce Paloise —
Egg yolks 3
Clarified butter, warm 200g
Fresh mint (not tarragon — paloise) 1 bunch
White wine vinegar, shallots 30ml / 2
— Ratatouille Niçoise —
Zucchini, eggplant, capsicum, tomato 150g each
Garlic, EVOO, thyme, basil as needed
Kalamata olive tapenade 60g
White Bean Purée — Make Morning Of
1.Cook soaked cannellini beans with garlic, bay, thyme until very tender — 45 min. No salt until cooked or skins toughen.
2.Drain, reserving cooking liquid. Blitz in blender with EVOO, lemon, truffle oil — add cooking liquid to reach silky, flowing consistency
3.Pass through fine sieve. Season. Keep warm in bain-marie. This is your carb — it must be luxurious.
Sauce Paloise (Mint Béarnaise)
1.Reduction: simmer shallots, white wine vinegar, crushed peppercorns until 1 tbsp remains. Strain.
2.Whisk yolks with reduction over bain-marie until ribbon stage — slow and patient
3.Drizzle warm clarified butter very slowly whisking constantly. Emulsify.
4.Finish with fresh mint chiffonade (not tarragon — this is paloise, the mint version). Keep warm.
Ratatouille Niçoise
1.Dice all vegetables uniform 1cm. Cook each separately in EVOO with garlic — never together or they turn to mush
2.Combine when cooked, add tomato concassé, thyme, torn basil. Season.
Lamb Rack — Service
1.Persillade: combine parsley, garlic, breadcrumbs, lemon zest, EVOO — should be vivid green and hold together
2.Season rack, sear hard on all sides in cast iron — deep crust, 3 min total
3.Brush bones and meat with Dijon. Press persillade firmly onto meat side.
4.Roast 200°C — 9 min for medium rare. Internal 57°C. Rest 5 min minimum.
5.Portion: 3 cutlets each, cut between bones with a clean decisive stroke
🍽️ Plating

Spoon white bean purée in a generous flowing pool to the left of the plate — use the back of a warm spoon to sweep it into an elegant arc, not a blob. Spoon ratatouille niçoise in a tight mound at centre. Rest three lamb cutlets upright against the ratatouille, bones pointing skyward — each cutlet leaning slightly for height and architecture. Small quenelle of olive tapenade behind the cutlets. Sauce paloise in a small warm jug poured tableside. Finish with a single sprig of fresh thyme and fleur de sel on the crust.

🌿 Condiments & Table Service
Sauce paloise — poured tableside in warm jug
Olive tapenade quenelle — on the plate
Fleur de sel — on the crust at plate-up
Extra sauce paloise in jug at table
Herb EVOO — 3 drops around the purée
Warm crusty bread — for the sauce
Chef's Note — Why White Bean Purée Instead of Potato

White bean purée with EVOO and a whisper of truffle oil is the more sophisticated choice for a fine dining context. It carries the lamb differently to potato — lighter on the palate, higher in protein (which owners notice), and it echoes the Provençal theme of the ratatouille. The sauce paloise (mint béarnaise) is Escoffier's recipe, made for lamb specifically. Every element on this plate has a reason to be there.

4 Portions Sauce: made fresh day-of Service time: 10 min GF option (no breadcrumbs)

Alternate Drop · Plate 03 of 03 · Chicken 04
🍗 Chicken · French Technique · 4 Portions
Chicken Ballotine — Porcini Cream
Truffle & herb mousse stuffing · Sauce suprême · Porcini mushroom cream · Asparagus · Condiments
Ingredients — 4 Portions
Free-range chicken breasts, skin on 4 large
— Truffle Mousse Stuffing —
Chicken thigh meat (mousse base) 200g
Thickened cream (cold) 80ml
Black truffle paste 20g
Fresh thyme, flat parsley as needed
Egg white 1
Salt, white pepper to season
— Porcini Cream —
Dried porcini mushrooms 40g
Fresh porcini or Swiss brown (if avail.) 200g
Shallots, finely diced 2
Garlic, thyme as needed
Dry white wine 100ml
Thickened cream 250ml
Chicken stock 150ml
Unsalted butter 40g
— Sauce Suprême —
Chicken stock, reduced 400ml
Cream, shallots, white wine 150ml / as needed
Cold unsalted butter (to mount) 80g
— Garnish —
Asparagus spears (pencil thin) 12 spears
Baby spinach or wilted watercress 100g
Truffle oil (finish) a few drops
Chicken Mousse — Make Morning Of
1.Blitz cold chicken thigh, egg white, salt in food processor until smooth paste
2.With processor running, drizzle in very cold cream — stop as soon as combined. Overworking = grainy.
3.Fold in truffle paste, chopped herbs by hand. Keep cold at all times.
Ballotine Assembly
1.Butterfly chicken breast: place flat, cut horizontally without separating — open like a book
2.Place a thin layer of mousse on the breast. Roll tight into a cylinder in clingfilm.
3.Twist ends tight like a bon-bon. Refrigerate minimum 2 hours to set shape.
4.Poach in 72°C water bath — exactly 18 minutes. Not 16. Not 20.
5.Remove clingfilm. Sear in foaming butter in hot pan — colour all sides, 2 min total. Rest 3 min.
Porcini Cream — 1 Hour Before Service
1.Rehydrate dried porcini in 200ml warm water 20 min — reserve soaking liquid, strain carefully
2.Sauté shallots and garlic in butter until soft. Add fresh mushrooms — cook until deeply golden
3.Deglaze with white wine, add porcini soaking liquid (careful — strain well), add stock + cream
4.Reduce by one third. Add rehydrated porcini. Season — earthy, rich, deep. Not thin.
5.Finish with cold butter swirled off heat — gloss and body
Sauce Suprême
1.Sweat shallots in butter, deglaze with white wine, reduce to almost nothing
2.Add reduced chicken stock + cream — simmer 10 min until nappe consistency (coats spoon)
3.Mount with cold butter off heat — velvety, ivory, elegant
Asparagus — To Order
1.Blanch in boiling salted water 90 seconds — refresh immediately in ice water
2.To order: finish in foaming butter 1 min, season, lemon squeeze
🍽️ Plating

Slice ballotine on a slight bias — two rounds per plate, showing the truffle mousse cross-section inside. Place in the centre of a warm wide plate, cut side facing up so the truffle layer is visible. Spoon porcini cream generously to one side — let it pool naturally, don't fight it. Sauce suprême in a thin elegant arc on the other side. Three asparagus spears stacked in a tight bundle alongside. Three drops of truffle oil on the porcini cream. Finish with a single small watercress sprig and a shaving of truffle if available. The cross-section of the ballotine is your hero — let them see it.

🍄 Condiments & Table Service
Sauce suprême — in a small warm jug, poured tableside
Porcini cream — generous pool on the plate
Truffle oil drops — on the porcini cream only
Fleur de sel — on the asparagus at plating
Shaved truffle (optional) — if budget allows, magnificent
Warm crusty bread — to absorb the sauce suprême
Chef's Note — Why Porcini Over Morel

Porcini is the right call here. Dried porcini carries an intensity — earthy, woody, almost meaty — that morel doesn't have. More importantly, the porcini soaking liquid becomes part of your sauce base, building depth you can't buy from a bottle. When you combine it with sauce suprême (the mother sauce of French chicken cookery), you get something that tastes like it took days to make. It didn't. But they don't need to know that.

4 Portions Mousse: morning of Poach: 72°C / 18 min precise Service time: 8 min

Live Carving Buffet Station & Dessert Programme 05

⚔️ Live Carving Station — A9 Wagyu Sirloin

The chef stands at the pass. The wagyu arrives whole, rested, crushed. Sliced tableside to order — thin, generous, glistening with marble. This is the centrepiece of the buffet. Everything else on this table exists to support it.

A9 WAGYU · HIGHEST MARBLE SCORE · CHEF CARVED TO ORDER
🔪 Live Carving Station

A9 Wagyu Sirloin

Whole roasted · Chef carved tableside

Season generously — salt and pepper only. Never mask the marble. Roast at 180°C to internal 52°C (rare-medium rare). Rest minimum 20 min under foil. Chef slices at station — 4–5mm slices, against the grain, per guest.

↑ The hero. Everything else is supporting cast.

Sauces at the Station

Three sauces · Guest choice

Sauce Bordelaise — red wine, bone marrow, shallot. Classic.
Wasabi cream — crème fraîche, fresh wasabi, lemon. Cuts the fat.
Chimichurri verde — parsley, EVOO, sherry vinegar, garlic. Bright and sharp.

↑ Three sauces = three personalities. Let owners choose.
🥗 Elevated Sides — Buffet Table

Pommes Dauphine

Crispy potato puffs · Chive crème fraîche

Choux pastry folded through duchess potato, piped and fried — light, airy, shatteringly crisp. A level above a roast potato. Served in a warm bowl with a side of chive crème fraîche for dipping.

↑ Nobody expects this at a hotel buffet. They will ask what it is.

Gratin Dauphinois

Slow-baked potato · Gruyère · Cream

Désirée potatoes sliced 3mm, layered with cream, garlic, thyme, gruyère. Baked covered 90 min, uncovered 20 min until golden and set. Portioned in individual cast iron dishes at the buffet — each guest gets their own.

↑ Individual cast iron = theatre + portion control.

Haricots Verts — Beurre Noisette

Fine green beans · Hazelnut butter · Almonds removed

Blanched fine beans finished in foaming beurre noisette with lemon juice and fleur de sel. Simple. Perfect. The nuttiness of the butter elevates a simple vegetable to something memorable.

NUT FREE — beurre noisette only, no almonds.

Heirloom Tomato & Burrata

Whole burrata broken tableside · Aged balsamic

Arrange heirloom tomatoes in a cascade of colours. Burrata placed whole at the centre — chef or server breaks it open tableside before the first guest. Aged balsamic drizzle, Ligurian EVOO, fleur de sel, basil oil, micro basil.

↑ The visual moment of the buffet table.

Roasted Asparagus

Thick spears · Parmesan · Lemon zest

Roasted at high heat until charred tips and tender stalks. Shaved parmesan over the top while hot. Lemon zest and EVOO to finish. Laid on the platter in one direction — clean, elegant, visually strong.

↑ Simplest dish on the table. Best-looking one.

Bread Station

Artisan · Warm · Three butters

Sourdough loaf sliced to order. Baguette. Focaccia rosemary. Three butters: salted cultured · truffle butter (for the wagyu moment) · herb compound butter. Warm bread = the first impression of the buffet table.

↑ Truffle butter next to the wagyu carving = deliberate pairing.

ACT 1 — Plated Petit Gâteau (Individual, Served to Table)

Valrhona Chocolate Fondant

Warm · Liquid centre · Crème anglaise

70% Valrhona bittersweet chocolate. Baked to order — 10 min at 190°C. Centre must flow. Crème anglaise poured tableside from a warm jug. Cocoa dusted. Gold leaf optional. This is the dessert that stops a conversation.

↑ Bake-to-order = the kitchen showing real confidence.

Lemon Tart — Brûléed

Classic French · Sharp · Torched tableside

Pâte sablée blind-baked. Filling: lemon juice, zest, eggs, cream, butter — set to a wobble. Dusted with fine sugar, brûléed with a torch at the table. The crack of the sugar crust is the moment. Crème fraîche and candied lemon.

↑ Tableside torch = drama. The sound is part of the dish.

Panna Cotta — Seasonal Fruit

Vanilla · Set delicately · Fresh berry coulis

Vanilla bean panna cotta, barely set — should tremble when the plate moves. Turned out onto the plate, not in a glass. Fresh seasonal berry coulis. Micro mint. Clean, elegant, dairy-forward. Best option for non-chocolate guests.

GF · Light · Palate cleanser after rich mains.
