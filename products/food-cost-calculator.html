Why food cost % is the most important number in your restaurant:

Food cost % tells you exactly how much of every dollar you earn goes back to buying ingredients. If your food cost is 30%, you keep 70 cents of every dollar before labour, rent, and utilities. If your food cost is 40%, you keep 60 cents — and most restaurants at 40% food cost are losing money.

Most first-time operators price their menu by gut feeling or by copying competitors. This calculator shows you the exact number for every dish — so you can price with confidence and build a menu that actually makes money.

Part 2 — Single Dish Calculator

Step-by-Step: Calculate One Dish
Use this method for every recipe on your menu
StepActionExample — Pad Thai
1List every ingredientRice noodles, egg, tofu, bean sprouts, spring onion, tamarind, fish sauce, peanuts, lime
2Weigh each ingredient per serve150g noodles, 1 egg, 80g tofu, 60g sprouts, 30g peanuts, 30ml sauce
3Calculate cost per serve$0.38 + $0.35 + $0.42 + $0.18 + $0.28 + $0.32 = $1.93
4Add 10% waste factor$1.93 x 1.10 = $2.12
5Divide by selling price$2.12 / $16.99 = 0.1248
6Multiply by 1000.1248 x 100 = 12.5% food cost
The 10% waste factor: Always add 10% to ingredient cost for trim waste, spillage, portioning variance, and spoilage. Restaurants that skip this consistently underestimate true food cost by 8–12%.

Every dish falls into one of four zones — know where each one sits

The quarterly menu review: Every 3 months, pull your sales data and map every dish to these 4 zones. Remove all Dogs. Promote all Stars. This single habit is worth more than any marketing spend.

Part 6 — Reverse Pricing Formula

Work Backwards From Your Target Food Cost
Use when building a new menu from scratch
// REVERSE FORMULA — minimum price to hit your target FC Minimum Sell Price = Ingredient Cost (with waste) / Target FC %

// EXAMPLES Ingredient cost $3.20, Target FC 22% — Min price = $3.20 / 0.22 = $14.55
Ingredient cost $5.80, Target FC 24% — Min price = $5.80 / 0.24 = $24.17
Ingredient cost $8.40, Target FC 26% — Min price = $8.40 / 0.26 = $32.31
Round up strategically: Never price at $14.55 — round to $14.99 or $15.99. That extra dollar drops food cost from 22% to 20.1%. On 50 covers a night that is roughly $20,000 extra per year from rounding alone.
