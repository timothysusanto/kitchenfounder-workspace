Day 1 — Indian / Mediterranean Theme
Lunch $55 · Dinner $72
🌶️
Existing Lunch: Roasted chicken — lemon herb spice  |  Existing Dinner: Butter chicken + Kashmiri beef rogan josh
🍽 Lunch Addition · Day 1 · Indian-Spiced
Tandoori-Style Grilled Salmon
Atlantic salmon fillets marinated in yoghurt, tandoori spice blend, lemon juice and ginger. Grilled on char grill for a charred exterior, tender pink centre. Served with cucumber raita and lemon wedges.
▸ Indian theme · complements the chicken · offers seafood alternative · yoghurt marinade = same profile as the existing Indian sides
GFHalal~36g protein
Key IngredientsSalmon fillet · full-fat yoghurt · tandoori spice blend · fresh ginger · garlic · lemon · cumin · coriander seed · chilli powder · turmeric · salt
MethodMarinate minimum 2 hrs. Char grill 4 min each side to 60°C internal. Rest 2 min. Serve with raita on the side. Portion at station — do not pre-slice.
Allergens: Fish · Dairy (yoghurt marinade + raita) · GF ✓
🍽 Dinner Addition · Day 1 · Indian-Spiced
Dal Tadka Grilled Chicken Thigh
Bone-in chicken thighs marinated in a dal tadka-inspired spice paste — turmeric, cumin, coriander, garlic, ginger and mustard seed temper in ghee. Grilled until charred, served with a small ladle of lentil tadka sauce over the top.
▸ Indian theme · different technique from butter chicken (grilled vs braised) · different protein cut · lentil sauce ties it into the vegetarian dal already on the menu
GFHalal~38g protein
Key IngredientsBone-in chicken thigh · ghee · turmeric · cumin · coriander · mustard seed · garlic · ginger · lentil tadka sauce (pre-made) · fresh coriander garnish
MethodMarinate 4 hrs minimum. Grill char grill medium-high. Finish oven 180°C to 74°C internal. Spoon warm dal tadka over at service. Garnish coriander leaves.
Allergens: Dairy (ghee) · GF ✓ · Halal ✓
Day 2 — Asian / Italian Theme
Lunch $55 · Dinner $72
🍜
Existing Lunch: Beef stir-fry — bok choy, ginger soy  |  Existing Dinner: Chicken cacciatore + Grilled salmon — lemon butter
🍽 Lunch Addition · Day 2 · Asian
Steamed Barramundi — Ginger, Soy & Spring Onion
Whole barramundi portions steamed to 60°C, finished tableside with hot ginger-infused oil poured over soy, fresh ginger julienne and spring onion. Classic Cantonese whole fish technique — clean, light, high-performance.
▸ Asian theme · different protein (fish vs beef) · steamed not stir-fried = lighter technique · Australian barramundi = local + sustainable · Cantonese method well-understood by Asian athletes
GFHalal~34g protein
Key IngredientsBarramundi portions · light soy sauce · sesame oil · fresh ginger julienne · spring onion · neutral oil for finishing pour · coriander
MethodSteam portions 8–10 min at 90°C. Place on serving tray. Lay ginger + spring onion on top. Heat neutral oil to smoking point, pour over ginger — it sizzles at table. Add soy + sesame oil. Serve immediately.
Allergens: Fish · Soy · Sesame · GF ✓
🍽 Dinner Addition · Day 2 · Italian
Osso Buco-Style Braised Lamb Shank
Lamb shank braised in tomato, white wine, garlic, carrot and celery for 3.5 hours until falling off the bone. Finished with gremolata (lemon zest, parsley, garlic). Served alongside the existing chicken cacciatore — both Italian braises, different proteins.
▸ Italian theme · same braised technique as cacciatore · lamb vs chicken = different protein choice · osso buco is the premium Italian braise — elevates the overall dinner perception
GFHalal~45g protein
Key IngredientsLamb shank · canned tomato · white wine · beef stock · garlic · carrot · celery · bay leaf · thyme · lemon zest · flat-leaf parsley (gremolata)
MethodSear shank all sides. Braise 3.5 hrs 160°C covered. Remove lid last 20 min to reduce sauce. Hold in braising liquid. Sprinkle gremolata at service. One shank per 2 portions.
Allergens: GF ✓ · Halal ✓ · DF ✓

Day 3 — Middle Eastern / European Theme
Lunch $55 · Dinner $72
🫒
Existing Lunch: Beef meatballs in tomato ragù  |  Existing Dinner: Slow cooked beef brisket + Chicken shawarma with hummus & baba ghanoush
🍽 Lunch Addition · Day 3 · Middle Eastern
Grilled Lamb Kofta — Harissa Yoghurt
Hand-formed lamb kofta with onion, parsley, cumin, coriander, smoked paprika and allspice. Grilled on skewers. Served with a harissa-spiked yoghurt sauce and warm flatbread. Complements the beef meatballs with a different protein and Middle Eastern spice profile.
▸ Middle Eastern theme · lamb vs beef = different protein · kofta + ragù meatballs = same mince technique, different culture · harissa yoghurt ties into the hummus/baba ghanoush already on the dinner menu
GFHalal~36g protein
Key IngredientsLamb mince · brown onion grated · flat-leaf parsley · cumin · coriander · allspice · smoked paprika · salt · skewers · harissa · Greek yoghurt for sauce
MethodMix kofta by hand — do not over-work. Form onto flat skewers 100g each. Grill char grill 3 min each side. Hold in oven 160°C until service. Serve 2 skewers per portion with harissa yoghurt alongside.
Allergens: Dairy (yoghurt sauce) · GF ✓ · Halal ✓
🍽 Dinner Addition · Day 3 · Middle Eastern
Za'atar Roasted Chicken Leg — Tahini Drizzle
Whole chicken legs roasted with za'atar, olive oil, lemon, garlic and sumac until skin is crispy and golden. Drizzled with a thin tahini sauce at service. Za'atar is the herb blend most associated with Levantine cooking — it bridges the Israeli, Lebanese and Syrian palates present at this event.
▸ Middle Eastern theme · chicken vs beef brisket and shawarma = third protein choice · za'atar + tahini = different flavour register from the shawarma spice blend · whole leg = dramatic plating at carving station
GFHalal~40g protein
Key IngredientsChicken leg · za'atar spice blend · olive oil · lemon juice + zest · garlic · sumac · salt · tahini sauce (tahini + lemon + garlic + water)
MethodScore chicken legs. Rub za'atar paste all over including under skin. Roast 180°C 45 min until skin golden + crispy. Rest 10 min. Carve or serve whole at station. Drizzle tahini at service — not in advance (tahini oxidises).
Allergens: Sesame (tahini) · GF ✓ · Halal ✓
Day 4 — Asian / Korean Theme
Lunch $55 · Dinner $72
🥢
Existing Lunch: Teriyaki chicken thigh fillet with Asian greens  |  Existing Dinner: Steamed barramundi — soy/ginger/shallots + Korean-style beef bulgogi
🍽 Lunch Addition · Day 4 · Korean-Inspired
Gochujang Glazed Pork Belly — Ssam Style
Thick pork belly slabs marinated in gochujang, soy, sesame oil, garlic and ginger. Roasted until caramelised, sliced and served with cos lettuce cups, steamed rice and a sesame dipping sauce for ssam-style wrapping. The interactive element creates conversation at the buffet.
▸ Korean theme · pork vs chicken = different protein · gochujang glaze is the defining Korean flavour · ssam-style service = interactive, familiar to Korean athletes, novel for others · complements teriyaki chicken without competing
GFHalal opt~35g protein
Key IngredientsHalal pork belly (or substitute beef short rib for full Halal) · gochujang paste · soy sauce · sesame oil · garlic · ginger · brown sugar · cos lettuce cups · sesame dipping sauce
MethodMarinate overnight. Roast 160°C 2 hrs. Increase to 200°C last 15 min for caramelised glaze. Rest 10 min. Slice 1cm thick at station. Serve with lettuce cups and rice on side for self-assembly.
Allergens: Soy · Sesame · GF ✓ · Note: substitute beef short rib if full Halal required
🍽 Dinner Addition · Day 4 · Asian
Five-Spice Duck Leg — Hoisin Glaze
Duck legs slow-roasted with Chinese five-spice, soy, ginger and a hoisin glaze applied in the final 15 minutes. Crispy skin, rich dark meat. Served with sliced spring onion and cucumber ribbons. Bridges the Korean theme with a broader pan-Asian profile — bold enough to stand next to bulgogi.
▸ Asian theme · duck vs fish and beef = third protein · five-spice + hoisin = Chinese influence that complements Korean flavours · duck is a premium protein — elevates the dinner station
GFHalal~38g protein
Key IngredientsDuck leg · five-spice powder · soy sauce · ginger · garlic · hoisin sauce · spring onion · cucumber · sesame oil
MethodScore duck skin. Rub five-spice + soy + ginger paste. Roast 160°C 1.5 hrs. Brush hoisin glaze last 15 min, increase to 200°C for crispy skin. Rest 10 min. Serve whole leg with garnish.
Allergens: Soy · Sesame · GF ✓ · Halal ✓

Day 5 — Mexican / Greek Theme
Lunch $55 · Dinner $72
🌮
Existing Lunch: Beef mince tacos with tortillas  |  Existing Dinner: Slow cooked lamb shoulder + tzatziki · Roasted chicken cutlet — Greek spices
🍽 Lunch Addition · Day 5 · Mexican
Achiote Grilled Chicken Breast — Pico de Gallo
Chicken breast marinated in achiote (annatto) paste, orange juice, cumin, garlic and oregano — the Yucatán cochinita pibil flavour profile applied to chicken. Grilled and served with fresh pico de gallo and corn tortillas alongside the existing beef tacos. Two taco proteins = more variety, same station.
▸ Mexican theme · chicken vs beef = second taco protein · achiote is the signature Yucatán spice — different flavour register from the beef mince · athletes can mix and match taco proteins · pico de gallo is already a condiment-familiar item
GFHalal~40g protein
Key IngredientsChicken breast · achiote paste · orange juice · lime · cumin · garlic · dried oregano · salt · pico de gallo (tomato, white onion, jalapeño, coriander, lime)
MethodMarinate 4 hrs minimum — achiote stains, use gloves. Butterfly thick breasts. Grill char grill 4 min each side to 74°C internal. Rest. Slice against grain. Serve at taco station alongside beef mince.
Allergens: GF ✓ · Halal ✓ · DF ✓
🍽 Dinner Addition · Day 5 · Greek
Lemon & Herb Grilled Barramundi — Olive Salsa Verde
Barramundi fillets marinated in lemon, olive oil, oregano, garlic and fresh thyme. Grilled skin-side first until crispy. Served with a Greek-style olive salsa verde — green olives, capers, anchovy, parsley and lemon. Light seafood option to balance the lamb shoulder and chicken on the existing menu.
▸ Greek theme · fish vs lamb and chicken = third protein for dinner · Greek cuisine has one of the strongest seafood traditions in Europe · olive salsa verde is Mediterranean · athletes wanting a lighter dinner option after 3 days of heavier proteins
GFHalal~34g protein
Key IngredientsBarramundi fillet skin-on · lemon · olive oil · dried oregano · fresh thyme · garlic · olive salsa verde (Kalamata olives, capers, anchovy, parsley, lemon, olive oil)
MethodScore skin. Marinate 1 hr max (lemon acid denatures fish quickly). Grill skin-side 3 min, flip 2 min. 60°C internal. Spoon olive salsa verde over at service. Do not pre-sauce — keep crispy skin.
Allergens: Fish · GF ✓ · Halal ✓ · Contains anchovy (fish)
