🇳🇱 Cultural Note — Why This Dinner
Erwtensoep is so central to Dutch culture it is simply called "snert" — a dish every Dutch athlete associates with cold winter evenings and family tables. Hachée is the Sunday stew of the Netherlands — braised beef with caramelised onions and cloves, slow-cooked for hours. The appeltaart is the Dutch national dessert, served in every café from Amsterdam to Rotterdam. Eating these dishes after a match in Sydney is psychological recovery as much as physical recovery.
Recovery Note · Beef shin delivers the highest collagen yield of any cut — ideal for joint recovery after a water polo match. Salmon omega-3 reduces inflammation. Split pea soup provides plant protein + fibre. Mashed potato replenishes glycogen fast. Appeltaart portion controlled — recovery carbs, not excess sugar.

Kitchen Production Notes — Dinner

Day 1 · Netherlands
  • Beef shin: start braise at 7am for evening service — 4hrs minimum at 160°C. Sear in batches first. Halal beer or substitute with extra beef stock + balsamic.
  • Erwtensoep: start 3hrs before service. Split peas need time. Blend half for texture — leave half whole.
  • Salmon: portion in AM. Bake to order in 10-min windows at 160°C. Do not overcook — 60°C internal only.
  • Appeltaart: bake morning of. Reheat portions at service. Serve warm with double cream.
Day 2 · Spain
  • Lamb shoulder: start at 6am — 5hrs at 150°C. Score and rub night before. Rest 30 min before carving. Carving station with rosemary garnish = theatre.
  • Gambas al ajillo: mise en place ready. Cook in 2-min batches to order. Keep warm in cazuela. Do not pre-cook — prawns overcook fast.
  • Crema catalana: set day before. Torch at table — have 2 blow torches + spare gas.
  • Churros: fry to order in small batches. Keep chocolate sauce warm in bain-marie.
Day 3 · USA
  • Brisket: start 8–10hrs before service in combi oven 120°C. Dry rub night before. Target core temp 90–95°C for full collagen breakdown. Rest minimum 45 min wrapped in butcher paper. Slice at station against the grain.
  • Buttermilk chicken: marinate 12hrs minimum. Grill to 74°C internal. Hold on rack, not in sauce — skin stays crispy.
  • Mac & cheese: make day before, bake to order in portions at 180°C for golden crust.
  • Brownies: bake same day. Serve warm. Ice cream scooped to order.
All 3 Dinners — Non-Negotiables
  • Zero nuts. Zero cross-contamination. Check every incoming delivery.
  • All proteins Halal-certified. Separate serving utensils at every station.
  • Hot proteins held minimum 63°C throughout service.
  • Soup served at minimum 75°C — check every 20 min.
  • Desserts labelled: allergens, dairy content, GF status.
  • Dinner service window: 19:00 — 21:00. Protein replenished at 20:00 if below 30%.
  • All carving stations staffed — no self-serve for large protein cuts.
