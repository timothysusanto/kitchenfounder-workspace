Kitchen Production Notes

Day 1 · Netherlands Kitchen Notes
  • Marinate chicken thighs minimum 4 hours prior in whole grain mustard + dill + garlic + lemon zest + olive oil
  • Grill on char grill · finish in oven 180°C to 74°C internal · rest 5 min · carve to order
  • Potato salad: boil baby potatoes until just tender · dress warm with apple cider vinegar, dill, white onion, Dijon, olive oil · serve at room temp
  • Braised red cabbage: start 2 hours before service (needs time)
  • No nuts anywhere on the line — confirm with all suppliers
Day 2 · Spain Kitchen Notes
  • Pollo al ajillo: brown chicken in batches · do not overcrowd · add sliced garlic (generous) + white wine + smoked paprika + bay · braise 35 min covered · finish uncovered 10 min to concentrate sauce
  • Saffron rice: bloom saffron in warm stock 10 min before adding — use real saffron not powder
  • Pisto: cook low and slow minimum 45 min — this is not a quick ratatouille, it needs time to sweeten
  • Pan con tomate salad: dress to order, not in advance — bread goes soggy fast
Day 3 · USA Kitchen Notes
  • House BBQ glaze: make day before — brown sugar + smoked paprika + apple cider vinegar + ketchup + garlic + black pepper · reduce to coating consistency
  • Airline breast: butterfly if over 200g for even cooking · grill 4 min each side · glaze last 90 seconds only (sugar burns) · rest 3 min · slice at carving station
  • Coleslaw: dress minimum 30 min before service so cabbage softens slightly
  • Baked beans: simmer low 90 min · check seasoning · must be rich and thick, not watery
  • Chocolate chip cookies: bake same morning, serve warm if possible
All 3 Days — Non-Negotiables
  • Zero nuts. Zero cross-contamination. Confirm every supplier every day.
  • All protein Halal-certified. Separate serving utensils at carving station.
  • Hot protein to be held at minimum 63°C until service ends.
  • Label everything — GF, VG, Halal, DF signs at every tray.
  • Service window: 12:00 — 14:00. Hot protein replenished at 13:00 if below 30%.
