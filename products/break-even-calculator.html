KitchenFounder — Break-Even Tips

Tip 1: Contribution margin is your most important number. Not revenue. Not profit. The contribution margin % tells you how much of every dollar of revenue is available to cover fixed costs and generate profit. A 37% CM means $0.37 from every dollar goes toward fixed costs. Increase your CM by reducing food cost % and labour % — even 2% improvement in each adds significant annual profit.
Tip 2: Break-even covers per day is your daily target. Print this number and stick it in the kitchen. If your break-even is 80 covers/day and you're doing 120, you're profitable. If you're doing 60, you have work to do. Track it daily, not monthly — by the time a bad month shows up in the numbers, 30 days have passed. Daily visibility = daily decisions.
Tip 3: Fixed costs are the trap — variable costs are the lever. Most operators focus on cutting food cost % but ignore fixed costs. A $500/month rent reduction is worth $6,000/year in permanent profit improvement — the same as serving 158 extra covers at $38 average spend. Negotiate rent before you sign. Negotiate again at renewal. Every dollar of fixed cost reduction goes straight to the bottom line, forever.
