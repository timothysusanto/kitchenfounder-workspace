💬 Service Scripts — Word For Word

1. Greeting a Table

"Good [evening], welcome to [Restaurant Name]. My name is [Name] and I'll be looking after you tonight. Can I start you with some still or sparkling water while you look through the menu?"

2. Taking an Order Professionally

"Are you ready to order, or would you like a few more minutes? ... Excellent choice. And how would you like the [steak/fish] cooked? ... Any dietary requirements I should let the kitchen know about? Perfect, I'll get that in for you right away."

3. Handling a Complaint (LAST Method)

Listen: "I'm so sorry to hear that — tell me exactly what happened."
Apologise: "That's completely unacceptable and I sincerely apologise."
Solve: "I'm going to [replace the dish / speak to the chef / remove this from your bill] right now."
Thank: "Thank you for letting us know — your feedback helps us get better."

4. Upselling Without Being Pushy

"The [dish] is really popular tonight — the kitchen has done something special with the sauce. A lot of guests have been pairing it with [wine recommendation]... if you're into that style."

5. Describing a Dish (Sensory Language)

"The wagyu burger is made with 200g of Marble Score 5 beef, grilled medium-rare, served with house-made aioli on a brioche bun. It's rich and juicy — it's been our best-seller since we opened."

6. Handling an Allergen Request

"Absolutely, thank you for letting me know. I'll go and check with the kitchen right now to confirm what's safe for you. I won't guess — I'll get you a confirmed answer. One moment please." [Check with chef. Return with clear yes/no — never a maybe.]

7. Closing a Table and Asking for Feedback

"I hope you had a wonderful evening. Before you go — is there anything we could have done even better for you tonight? ... Thank you so much. We'd love to see you again. If you get a chance to leave us a Google review, it means the world to a small team like ours."

8. Handling a No-Show Reservation

[Phone call to guest, 15 minutes after booking time:] "Hi, this is [Name] from [Restaurant]. I'm just calling to check on your reservation for [time] tonight — we have your table ready for you. Let me know if your plans have changed and I can hold it a little longer or release it for another guest. No worries either way — just give us a quick call back. Thank you!"
