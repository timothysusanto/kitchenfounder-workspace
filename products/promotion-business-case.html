Case 1 of 4 — Junior Sous Chef · Banquet Kitchen03
Praveen Dwaselaan
Proposed: Junior Sous Chef — Banquet Kitchen · Novotel
PROPOSED SALARY $76,000 / year
equiv. $36.54/hr

Position Background

This proposal fills the vacant Junior Sous Chef position previously held by Ronald Caqui in the Banquet Kitchen at Novotel Sydney Olympic Park. The position has been vacant and the operational gap is directly impacting the quality and consistency of Banquet and Conference & Events (C&E) delivery. The Banquet Kitchen is responsible for generating approximately $2.9 million in annual revenue through corporate events, conferences, weddings, and major sporting hospitality. This revenue stream requires dedicated, experienced leadership at the Junior Sous Chef level to maintain operational standards and client satisfaction.

Why Praveen

  • Demonstrated ability to elevate the quality and presentation of C&E food offerings through his distinct culinary approach and technical skill
  • Formally acknowledged by his peers and the broader F&B team for raising the standard of Banquet and C&E food quality
  • Received unsolicited compliments from visiting sports teams, which were shared publicly in the hotel WhatsApp group chat — a rare and genuine indicator of guest-facing impact
  • Has the temperament, work ethic, and kitchen presence required to lead a Banquet team during high-volume event days
  • Filling this role internally eliminates recruitment cost, onboarding time, and performance uncertainty

Business Justification

The Banquet Kitchen operates at its most critical during large-scale C&E events, which can serve 300–600 covers in a single session. Without a Junior Sous Chef in the Banquet Kitchen, the Head Chef is required to absorb operational oversight that should be delegated — reducing strategic capacity and increasing execution risk on high-revenue event days. Praveen's promotion directly addresses this structural gap. His salary at $76,000 per year represents less than 2.7% of the annual Banquet revenue this role is responsible for supporting.

Supplementary Proposal — Staffing Agency Partnership06

Moving Away from Sidekicker — A Case for Consistent Agency Partnership

The current reliance on Sidekicker for casual kitchen labour has created inconsistency in staff quality, unpredictable skill levels on event days, and a lack of accountability that does not meet the standard required for a $2.9M banquet operation. A dedicated staffing agency relationship provides vetted, consistent, trained casual labour with accountability on both sides.

The Sheraton Grand Sydney Hyde Park operates an F&B revenue of approximately $24 million per year. Their continuity of casual staffing quality — achieved through a formal agency partnership — is a benchmark we should be working toward. I am recommending we request a meeting with their F&B leadership to understand their agency model and replicate it here.

Why Sidekicker Is Not Enough
  • ✗ No guaranteed repeat staff — different faces every shift
  • ✗ No culinary vetting — skill levels vary widely
  • ✗ No relationship with the worker — no accountability
  • ✗ App-based platform not designed for professional kitchen environments
  • ✗ Cannot handle volume requirements of Water Polo World Cup
What a Dedicated Agency Provides
  • ✓ Consistent pool of pre-vetted kitchen staff who know our operation
  • ✓ Account manager relationship — direct escalation when issues arise
  • ✓ Reference-checked, culinary-qualified candidates
  • ✓ Volume capability for large events (50–100+ pax event days)
  • ✓ Temp-to-perm pathway — agency staff can be assessed for permanent roles
Sheraton Grand Sydney — Reference & Benchmark Meeting

The Sheraton Grand Sydney Hyde Park operates a total F&B revenue of approximately $24 million per year. Their ability to maintain consistent casual staffing standards across high-volume banquet and events operations is a model directly applicable to our context. I am requesting approval to:

Action 1
Arrange a reference meeting with Sheraton Grand Sydney F&B leadership to understand their agency staffing model and identify preferred partners
Action 2
Identify 2–3 specialist hospitality staffing agencies in Sydney (e.g. Trippas White Group suppliers, Pinnacle People, Allstaff) and request proposals
Action 3
Establish preferred agency by 30 June 2026 — in place and briefed before Water Polo World Cup commences 19 July 2026
