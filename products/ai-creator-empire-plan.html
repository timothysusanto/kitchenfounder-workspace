The Philosophy · 5 Inner Human Desires02
Why This Works: The Human Architecture of Attention
Music is where humans spend the most time. Not food videos. Not news. Music. A person listens to 32 minutes of music per day on average — Spotify data confirms this. They watch 19 minutes of YouTube. They scroll TikTok for 95 minutes. But at the core of all three, when the algorithm works perfectly, is the same thing: an emotional state the human was seeking and couldn't find until the content found them.

Every sustainable content business is built not on a format (video, audio, text) but on a desire. Humans have five core desires that drive the majority of media consumption time. Build a persona for each desire. Feed that desire better than anyone else. Scale to every platform where that desire exists. That is the architecture of a $100 billion content company.

The five desires are universal. They cross languages, cultures, ages. An elderly woman in Bangkok and an elderly woman in São Paulo both fear the same things about their bodies. A young man in Lagos and a young man in Jakarta both carry the same private hopes. Demographics are geography. Desires are universal. Build for desires — you build for the world.

The MSTR + Music Philosophy

Michael Saylor doesn't explain Bitcoin. He invokes it. He makes people feel that they are either in possession of a profound truth or being left behind by it. That is emotional marketing at its highest form — it operates like music. The best AI content works the same way: it doesn't explain the desire, it invokes it. A 60-second AI health video that makes a 68-year-old woman feel capable and seen is operating at the same frequency as a great song. The goal is not to inform — it is to make the audience feel something true about themselves. That is where the engagement, the subscription, the loyalty, and ultimately the revenue live.

Why Music Changes Everything
Music is consumed for 32 minutes per person per day globally — more than any other content format. Spotify has 600M+ users. YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Videos all play music. Every piece of AI-generated music you own is a royalty-generating asset that earns money while you sleep, on every platform, forever. The AI music stack costs $30–50/month to generate. The royalty potential is unlimited.

The MSTR strategy applied to music: create assets, not content. A viral TikTok has a lifespan of 48 hours. A Spotify song earns forever. Every persona has a music identity. Every music identity becomes a Spotify artist page. Every Spotify artist page becomes a distribution channel that supports the video content. Music is not a side project — it is the foundation layer that makes every other platform more powerful.
