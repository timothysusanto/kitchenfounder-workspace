The Surprise Philosophy

At Michelin level, the greatest restaurants are remembered not for the menu alone — but for the moment. The unexpected flavour. The gesture that says: we saw you, we thought of you.

We have 8 days and 16 services to give 400 athletes from 16 nations something they will talk about long after the medals are awarded. Every dinner holds one surprise element — a dish, a presentation, a touch of theatre — that nobody saw coming. That is the edge that separates a good event from an unforgettable one.

Athlete Nutrition Standards

  • Hot quality protein minimum 2 sources per service
  • Complex carbohydrates at every service
  • Maximum 3 salads per dinner — quality over quantity
  • Fresh fruit as primary dessert — athlete-focused
  • Winter soup at every dinner — warmth & recovery
  • Live beverage station all services
  • Sandwich station at every lunch — build-your-own, athlete-customisable
  • Allergen info displayed at every station

Food Safety — Non-Negotiable

  • 100% NUT FREE — all 16 services, no exceptions
  • No cold/cured meats — food poisoning risk eliminated
  • No raw protein — no tartare, no sushi
  • All hot food above 63°C throughout service
  • Max 2-hour buffet window — replaced, never topped up
  • Temperature log completed every service
  • Head Chef or Sous Chef present entire service

The food will fuel them. The moments will define us.

Inclusivity & Dietary Programme 08
Every Nation · Every Athlete · Every Need
A Table for
All 16 Nations
True hospitality means nobody feels like an afterthought. Whether an athlete eats halal, avoids gluten, follows a plant-based diet, or keeps kosher — they will find food made with the same care and skill as everything on this menu. No separate table. No inferior option. Just good food for everyone.

🕌 Halal

HALAL

Halal protein options available at every service. Available on 48-hour pre-notification through team managers. All halal proteins sourced from certified Sydney suppliers, prepared on separate equipment, served with dedicated utensils and clear station labelling.

✡️ Kosher

KOSHER

Kosher meals prepared by an approved certified Sydney supplier and delivered sealed. Available on 72-hour pre-notification through team managers. Served with original sealed packaging and a plated presentation — not a compromise, a commitment.

🌾 Gluten Free

GF

Gluten-free options available at every service — clearly labelled. Includes: grilled proteins, roasted vegetables, rice, fresh fruit, yoghurt, salads without croutons. Cross-contamination precautions taken at prep stage. GF bread available on request.

🥗 Vegetarian & Vegan

VVG

Vegetarian option at every service. Vegan option on 24-hour pre-notification. All plant-based dishes are substantive — not sides dressed as mains. Includes: gnocchi, tofu stir fry, vegetable tagine, frittata, pasta napolitana, cannelloni, curry, and more.

🥛 Dairy Free

DF

Dairy-free options available at every service. Oat milk, soy milk, and coconut milk available at the beverage station. Clearly labelled options throughout the buffet. Athletes with dairy intolerance will never feel limited at this table.

🚫 Nut Free (All Services)

NUT FREE

Zero nuts in any form across all 16 services — no exceptions. No peanuts, tree nuts, nut oils, nut-based sauces. All recipes reviewed. All suppliers confirmed. All kitchen team briefed. Allergen boards at every station. This is a non-negotiable commitment.

🐟 Low FODMAP & High Protein

ATHLETE

The entire menu is designed around high-protein, complex carbohydrate principles for elite athletic performance. Athletes with specific dietary protocols — low FODMAP, high-protein targets — can meet team nutrition managers to pre-arrange customised plates.

🌍 Cultural Menus

16 NATIONS

The 8-day rotation intentionally moves through Western, Italian, Asian, Greek, and Middle Eastern themes — so every nation encounters at least one night of familiar flavour. No one is a guest at someone else's dinner. Every team is the guest of honour.

Pre-Event Dietary Registration: Team managers are requested to submit dietary requirements (halal, kosher, vegan, GF, dairy-free, other medical needs) at least 72 hours before arrival via the event coordinator. Every request is treated as a priority — not an exception. Our kitchen will prepare with the same care and skill for all. No athlete should ever feel like an afterthought at this table.
