Standard Canapé Package — 5 Pieces Per Person

As detailed in the Novotel SOP Conference & Events Kit. Package includes 2 cold canapés, 2 hot canapés, and 1 substantial canapé. Chef's selection based on seasonality and dietary requirements of your group. Full canapé menu available on request. Minimum 72 hours notice for dietary modifications.

Why Novotel SOP Over Pullman Sydney Hyde Park

Given AW Edwards' construction sites are predominantly near Sydney Olympic Park, Novotel SOP provides significantly easier access for delegates — avoiding CBD traffic and parking constraints. Ample complimentary parking on-site, direct train access via Olympic Park Station (15 min from CBD), and purpose-built conference infrastructure in Freshwater 1 & 2.
