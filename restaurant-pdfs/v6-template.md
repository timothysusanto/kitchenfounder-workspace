# v6 Design System — Tokyo Convenience Store Aesthetic

## Color Palette (Global)
- Body background: #0a0a0a
- Page background: #111111
- Primary text: #e8e8e8
- Secondary text: #999999
- Border color: #2a2a2a
- Gold accent: #ffd700 (prices, profit numbers)
- Each cuisine has its own NEON accent color (see per-guide specs)

## Typography
- Headings: 'Space Grotesk', system-ui, sans-serif (Google Fonts)
- Body: 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif
- Numbers/costs: 'DM Mono', monospace (makes food costs look like data)
- Google Fonts import: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Inter:wght@300;400;500;600&family=DM+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap

## Cover Design
- Full dark background page
- Cuisine neon accent color for h1
- Thin horizontal rule in neon color
- Eyebrow: small caps, letter-spacing 4px, neon color
- Tagline: grey italic
- KPI row: dark cards with neon accent borders

## Recipe Cards
- Dark card: background #1a1a1a, border 1px solid #2a2a2a
- Recipe header: solid neon accent color background, dark text
- Instead of photo: COLOR BLOCK — gradient from neon to #0a0a0a, height 80px, with dish name centered in it
- Ingredient table: dark rows alternating #1a1a1a / #141414
- Food cost box: dark bg, neon border, gold numbers
- Steps: numbered with neon circles
- Chef tip: dark amber box

## Chef's Lens Section (replaces buyer story)
TWO PARTS:

PART 1 — "What a chef sees" (technical Michelin perspective)
- Dark card with neon left border
- Heading: "What a Michelin-trained chef sees in [cuisine]"
- 2-3 paragraphs of genuine technical respect for the cuisine
- Specific technique, specific ingredient, specific business insight
- Voice: direct, earned, no bullshit

PART 2 — "What this guide does"  
- Separate dark card
- Heading: "What happens when you read this"
- 1-2 paragraphs about the emotional/business shift
- Generic — no names, no locations, no fake people
- Ends with a punchy one-liner about the blueprint

## Tone (Gen Z / Michelin brain hybrid)
- No corporate language
- No "we are pleased to present"
- Short sentences. Active voice. Real talk.
- Numbers are the heroes — display them prominently
- "The food cost is 11%. That's the number. Everything else is technique."
