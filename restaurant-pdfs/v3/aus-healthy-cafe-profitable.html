Method — Avocado

1
Halve and stone avocado. Scoop flesh into a bowl. Add lemon juice, olive oil, and sea salt. Smash with a fork — not a blender. You want chunky texture with some whole pieces, not a smooth paste. Over-mashing = baby food. The texture is what you're selling.

Method — Poached Egg

2
Fill a wide saucepan with 8cm of water. Add a splash of white vinegar. Bring to a gentle simmer — small bubbles only, not a rolling boil. Crack egg into a small cup. Create a gentle swirl in the water with a spoon. Lower the cup and slide the egg in. Cook 3 minutes for a runny yolk, 3.5 for jammy. Remove with a slotted spoon. Drain on paper towel.
3
Toast sourdough until deeply golden — not pale. Spread smashed avo generously. Place poached egg on top. Arrange cherry tomatoes. Scatter microgreens, dukkah, chilli flakes, and pomegranate. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and flaked sea salt. The visual should look effortless but deliberate.
💡 Avocado ripeness is everything. Order avocados 3 days before you need them and ripen at room temperature. Refrigerate once ripe — holds 2 more days. Check every single avocado before service. A brown, oxidised avo on the plate costs you more in reputation than the $1.20 it costs to replace it.
