4Staff Forecasting

Australian healthy cafés command higher average spend ($55–80/head) which improves revenue per cover. A 35–45 seat café targeting $5,500–$7,500 daily can run lean on labour while maintaining premium service standards through smart mise en place systems.

💡 Chef's Tip: Australian healthy cafés typically operate breakfast and lunch only (7am–3pm). This dramatically reduces labour cost — no dinner shift means no split shifts, lower overtime, and better staff retention. Run 5.5 days and close Sunday afternoon for cleaning and prep.

5Kitchen Workflow

🔥 Hot Section

  • 1× commercial flat top grill (eggs, fritters)
  • 1× 6-burner range (frittata, sauces)
  • 1× convection oven (banana bread, frittata)
  • 1× deep fryer (wedges, corn fritters)
  • Bain marie for batch sauces

❄️ Cold & Prep Section

  • High-speed blender station (smoothie bowls)
  • Açaí/smoothie prep fridge
  • Display fridge (chia pots, energy balls)
  • Dedicated sourdough slicing station
  • Espresso bar — premium machine essential

☕ The Coffee Rule — Your Most Profitable Item

  • Flat white / oat latte at $6–7: food cost 8–12% — highest margin item on the menu
  • Target 2 coffees per cover — train staff to offer second coffee naturally
  • Specialty coffee programme (single origin, seasonal) justifies $7–8 price point
  • Add cold brew, matcha latte, turmeric latte — all at 8–15% food cost
  • Coffee alone can represent 35–40% of your total revenue
