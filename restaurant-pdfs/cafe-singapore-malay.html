Why Singapore & Malay? Southeast Asian cuisine is one of the fastest-growing segments in US dining. Bold spices, rich coconut-based curries, and hawker-style dishes offer high flavour impact at accessible price points. This format thrives in urban markets, food halls, and casual dining strips.

5Kitchen Workflow

🔥 Hot Section

  • 2× high-BTU wok burners (essential)
  • 1× charcoal grill for satay/ayam bakar
  • 1× deep fryer (roti, fritters, tempeh)
  • 1× large stockpot for laksa broth
  • 1× rice cooker station (×3 commercial)

❄️ Cold & Prep Section

  • Spice paste prep area (blender station)
  • Marinating fridge (24–48hr cycles)
  • Cendol shaved ice machine
  • Kuih/dessert display fridge
  • Popiah rolling station
