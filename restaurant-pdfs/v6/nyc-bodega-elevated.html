// What a Michelin-trained chef sees in the NYC bodega format
The bodega is the most honest food institution in New York City. Open at 5am. Feeds everyone. No pretension. The bacon egg and cheese on a Kaiser roll is one of the most technically interesting sandwiches in American food — the egg folded just so, the cheese melted through with steam from the flat-top, the roll toasted on the same surface. It takes 4 minutes and requires a specific sequence that every bodega worker learns by feel. Elevating that format means respecting the original: keeping the speed, the value, the accessibility — and improving ingredient quality and technique without losing the soul. The chopped cheese is not a burger. Do not make it into a burger.
// What this guide does
The bodega elevated concept works because it takes a format that New Yorkers trust emotionally and adds the execution quality the original format never had resources for. The café Bustelo cortadito at $3.99 costs under $0.50 to make. The banana pudding at $8.99 with an 8% food cost closes the ticket. The $28 average spend in a 30-seat room with minimal labour produces margins that most full-service restaurants cannot match.
This guide shows you how to run it.

1
Toast brioche cut-side down on flat-top with butter. 90 seconds. Move to corner of grill to keep warm.
2
Crack eggs directly onto flat-top at 180°C. Break yolks. Fold into rectangle roughly the size of the roll. Season.
3
Lay cheese slices over egg. Cover with dome or foil for 30 seconds to melt through with steam. Do not skip this step — the steam melt is the technique.
4
Add bacon to bottom bun. Slide egg-cheese stack on top. Close. Wrap in foil for 20 seconds — it steams further and holds together. Serve immediately.
💡 The foil wrap is the bodega secret. It steams everything together and means the customer gets it hot even if they walk out. 20 seconds in foil = better sandwich.

Chopped Cheese — Bodega Classic
8 min ticket · FC: 19% · $14.99 · SIGNATURE
IngredientQtyCost
Ground beef (80/20)180g$1.40

1
Cook onions on flat-top until soft. Add beef, season. Cook breaking apart with spatula. When nearly done, chop repeatedly with spatula — you're creating a crumbled, chopped texture, not a patty.
2
Lay cheese slices over meat. Cover with dome 30 seconds to melt completely into the meat mixture.
3
Toast hero roll on flat-top. Sauce bottom half (ketchup, mayo). Add meat-cheese mixture. Top with lettuce and tomato. Wrap tight in foil.
💡 The chopping motion is essential. It combines the beef, onion and cheese into one unified filling. A burger patty on a roll is not a chopped cheese. The texture is the identity.
Birria Quesadilla + Consommé
Birria Quesadilla — Consommé Dip
10 min ticket · FC: 21% · $14.99 · SIGNATURE
IngredientQtyCost
Beef birria (braised chuck, chiles)150g$1.80
Consommé (birria braising liquid)120ml$0.40
Flour tortillas2 large$0.30
Oaxacan cheese, shredded60g$0.90
White onion + cilantro, fine chop20g each$0.15
1
Birria prep (batch, day before): Braise beef chuck with guajillo, ancho, pasilla chiles, tomato, onion, garlic, cumin, oregano at 160°C for 4 hours. Pull meat, strain braising liquid (this is the consommé).
2
To order: dip tortilla in consommé fat layer. Place on hot flat-top. Add cheese, birria meat, onion-cilantro. Fold, press. Cook 2 min each side until crispy and cheese melts through.
3
Serve with ramekin of hot consommé for dipping. Dip, eat, repeat.
💡 The consommé dip is the dish. Without it, it's a quesadilla. With it, it's an experience. Keep the consommé hot and well-seasoned — it gets tasted and it gets Instagrammed.

SECTION 5 — SERVICE TRAINING

Script 01 — Welcome
"The food is bodega. The technique isn't. Everything here is made from scratch — the cheese sauce, the consommé, the flan. Same energy, different standard. The chopped cheese and the BEC are the moves."
Script 02 — Chopped Cheese
"The chopped cheese is chopped on the flat-top — beef, onion, American cheese melted through, ketchup, mayo on a hero roll. It's a New York thing. You won't find it outside the five boroughs. We do it right."
Script 03 — Birria Quesadilla
"The birria is beef braised in chiles for 4 hours. The consommé on the side is the braising liquid. You dip the quesadilla in, eat it, repeat. The dip is the whole point."
Script 04 — Café Bustelo Cortadito
"The cortadito is Cuban espresso with sweetened condensed milk. $3.99. Stronger than anything at Starbucks. It's how the bodega opens every morning in New York."
Script 05 — Return Visit
"The big breakfast plate on weekends — two eggs, meat of your choice, toast, home fries. Different energy. Same quality. Come back Saturday."
