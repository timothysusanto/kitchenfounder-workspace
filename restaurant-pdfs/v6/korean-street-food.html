// What a Michelin-trained chef sees in Korean cooking
Korean fermentation is a living process, not a recipe. Kimchi is not made — it is started. The lacto-fermentation that transforms salted napa cabbage, gochugaru, garlic and ginger into kimchi takes days, weeks, months depending on temperature and desired flavour profile. The kimchi in this guide is made in-house because the commercial alternative is a different product. In-house kimchi at 3 weeks fermentation has a complexity that no factory product replicates. Gochujang — fermented chili paste aged for months — is the backbone of Korean flavour. Making it from scratch is a commitment. It is also a competitive moat. Your gochujang is yours. Nobody else's kitchen tastes like yours.
// What this guide does
Korean food in America is having its defining decade. The operators who will own this market long-term are not the ones following the trend — they are the ones making it properly, with fermented kimchi, house gochujang and jjigae that takes three hours. The food cost on kimchi jjigae is 13.8%. The differentiation it creates is incalculable.
This guide is for operators who want to do it right.

Chef's Lens

// What a Michelin-trained chef sees in Korean cooking
Korean fermentation is a living process, not a recipe. Kimchi is not made — it is started. The lacto-fermentation that transforms salted napa cabbage, gochugaru, garlic and ginger into kimchi takes days, weeks, months depending on temperature and desired flavour profile. The kimchi in this guide is made in-house because the commercial alternative is a different product. In-house kimchi at 3 weeks fermentation has a complexity that no factory product replicates. Gochujang — fermented chili paste aged for months — is the backbone of Korean flavour. Making it from scratch is a commitment. It is also a competitive moat. Your gochujang is yours. Nobody else's kitchen tastes like yours.
// What this guide does
Korean food in America is having its defining decade. The operators who will own this market long-term are not the ones following the trend — they are the ones making it properly, with fermented kimchi, house gochujang and jjigae that takes three hours. The food cost on kimchi jjigae is 13.8%. The differentiation it creates is incalculable.
This guide is for operators who want to do it right.
