7Weekly Prep Schedule

Prep philosophy: A roti shop kitchen runs on daily freshness and disciplined batch cooking. The green seasoning, shadow beni chutney, and callaloo must be made fresh. The stews — goat, oxtail, pepper pot, and chicken — are batch-cooked and held. The roti dough is made fresh daily. The key discipline is the overnight braise: oxtail and goat go in at closing, cook overnight, and are ready for service the next day.

Critical hiring note: The tawa cook is the most specialised role. Making dhalpuri roti — kneading, filling, rolling, and cooking on a hot tawa — is a skill that takes weeks to develop to service standard. Hire someone with roti shop experience or plan 3–4 weeks of intensive tawa training before opening. A head chef who cannot make dhalpuri roti cannot run a roti shop, regardless of other culinary skills.
