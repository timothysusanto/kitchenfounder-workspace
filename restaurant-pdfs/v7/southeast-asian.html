🌏 Concept Identity

This guide covers the six culinary traditions that define Southeast Asian cuisine: Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino, Indonesian, Singaporean, and Malaysian. The goal is not fusion — it's honest, technique-driven execution of distinct regional classics at NYC dinner standard pricing ($55–$95 per person).

The golden rule: every dish must taste like it came from the street — not a hotel. High technique, humble soul.

⚠️ NUT-FREE POLICY — MANDATORY

This kitchen operates as 100% tree nut and peanut free. Every traditional nut-based preparation has been reformulated:

  • Satay sauce: Sunflower seed butter + coconut cream
  • Kare-Kare sauce: Pumpkin seed paste (pepita) + annatto
  • Pad Thai: No peanut garnish — crispy shallots + sesame
  • Gado-gado style: Sunflower seed dressing
  • All desserts: No praline, no marzipan

Staff must complete allergen briefing before first service. Update all menus and table tents accordingly.

Massaman paste: dried chilies, lemongrass, galangal, shallots, garlic, toasted spices — no nuts.

  1. Sear lamb in batches — hot pan, no overcrowding. Deep caramelization is key. Set aside.
  2. Fry massaman paste in coconut cream (thick top layer from can) 3–4 min until oil pools on surface.
  3. Return lamb, add coconut milk, cardamom, cinnamon, tamarind. Bring to simmer.
  4. Braise covered 90 min until lamb is almost tender. Add potatoes and shallots.

  6. Finish with fish sauce and palm sugar balance. Serve with jasmine rice and roti.
💡 Chef Tip: Traditional massaman uses peanuts and cashews. This version omits them entirely — the rich, thick gravy from coconut cream and potato starch provides all the body needed. Add toasted pumpkin seeds as garnish for texture contrast.

🎭 Service Scripts

Chilli Heat Education

"At [Restaurant], our chillis are fresh and authentic. Here's how we guide you:"

For first-timers: "I'd recommend mild or medium — our Thai chillis are much hotter than most guests expect. Our green curry is a great starting point."


For heat-seekers: "Our Isaan-style som tum is pounded to authentic street-food heat. We can adjust — just let me know."


If a guest receives hot food by accident: "I'm so sorry — please let me bring you fresh jasmine rice immediately. It neutralizes heat much better than water."


Selling up: "The whole fish at medium heat is our most ordered dish — the chilli lime sauce is bright, not overwhelming."

Family-Style Sharing

Southeast Asian dining is communal. Train staff to facilitate this naturally:

Recommend sharing: "Our dishes are designed for the table to share — I'd suggest 2–3 mains for 4 people, plus starters and rice for the table."


Timing coordination: "We'll send all dishes together so you can share family-style, is that okay? Or would you prefer courses?"


Rice framing: "Jasmine rice is the anchor — we'll bring one per two guests as a base."


Upsell moment: "For 4 people, the whole fish is a beautiful centerpiece — it's meant to be shared and presented tableside."

Coconut Milk Dessert Script

For mango sticky rice tableside and cendol:

Mango sticky rice intro: "This is our signature dessert — I'll slice the mango and pour the coconut cream here at the table for you."


During presentation: "The sticky rice absorbs the coconut cream over a few minutes — let it rest briefly before eating, the flavors develop."


Coconut cracking: "I'll crack this young coconut at the table for you. The water inside is fresh and naturally sweet — then you can scoop the flesh with the spoon."


Dietary note: "All our desserts are nut-free and most are gluten-free — just let me know if you have any other dietary needs."

🌿 NUT-FREE Kitchen Certification Path

Operating as a 100% nut-free kitchen is a major competitive differentiator in NYC. These steps take you there:

  1. Audit all suppliers — request allergen certificates
  2. Replace all peanut/tree nut products with approved substitutes
  3. Label all containers clearly (green = nut-free verified)
  4. Staff allergen training — full team, documented
  5. Menu and website updated with NF certification
  6. Contact local allergy support groups — they refer restaurants
  7. Consider FARE (Food Allergy Research & Education) partnership

Estimated reach: 32 million Americans have food allergies. NF certification = untapped market.
