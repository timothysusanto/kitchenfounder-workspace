🎯 Concept DNA

  • Dry-aged USDA Prime beef — minimum 28 days
  • Tableside theatre: porterhouse carving, bone marrow, bananas foster flambé
  • Vintage steakhouse aesthetic — dark wood, leather, brass
  • Power-dining atmosphere — NYC financial/media crowd
  • The steak IS the experience; everything else amplifies it

💡 Operating Principles

  • Food cost target: 28–32% blended
  • Labor cost target: 30–33%
  • Beverage mix: 35% of total revenue
  • Table turn: 1.4x per night (90 min avg)
  • Dry-age cabinet front-of-house — visible to guests
  • Signature: Peter Luger-style porterhouse carved tableside

🥩 Beef Program

  • Grade: USDA Prime, hormone-free, Angus or Wagyu blend
  • Dry-age: 28-day minimum; 45-day for special cuts
  • Butchery: In-house; full primal breakdown
  • Display: Front-of-house aging cabinet (temp-controlled, 34–38°F)
  • Waste protocol: Trim → burger / bone broth / staff meal

🎭 Signature Moments

  • Porterhouse: Chef carves tableside on wooden board (Peter Luger DNA)
  • Bone Marrow: Presented tableside with toast points + sea salt
  • Bananas Foster: Flambé at table — showstopper dessert
  • Dry-age reveal: Sommelier walks guests past aging cabinet on arrival

Operations
Kitchen Design & Service Scripts

🔥 Kitchen Design — Grill Station Dominance

  • Grill Station (Primary): 2× commercial char-broiler (900°F), cast-iron flat-top, salamander broiler. This is the heart — position facing expeditor.
  • Cold Station: Directly adjacent — oyster shucking, tartare, chilled starters. Refrigerated rail at 38°F.
  • Sauté Station: For sauces, sides finishes, lobster mac reheat. 6-burner range + tilt skillet.
  • Dry-Age Cabinet: Front-of-house walk-in display window (glass-front, 34–38°F, humidity 80%). Guests see the aging program — it's marketing.
  • Butchery: Separate room with band saw, walk-in cooler, vacuum sealer. Daily primal breakdown 2:00–4:00 PM.
  • Expeditor: Central pass with heat lamps, steak temp probe station, verbal call system.
  • Flambé/Guéridon: Side station near dining room entry — gas burner, copper pan, mise en place.

📋 Daily Prep Schedule

10:00 AMButchery: break down primals, portion steaks, vacuum-seal. Inspect dry-age cabinet temps.
11:00 AMStocks: beef stock + bone broth on. Caramelize onion batch (10 lbs). Roast bones for marrow (refrigerate until service).
12:00 PMCold prep: tartare mix prep (hold components separate until order). Wedge salads prepped, wrapped. Cocktail sauce made fresh.
1:00 PMSauces: bordelaise, béarnaise, horseradish cream, truffle butter, compound butters. Portion and label all.
2:00 PMDesserts: set chocolate mousse, slice cheesecake, prep bananas foster mise en place (tableside cart stocked).
3:00 PMSides: batch hash brown base, batter onion rings. Portion mac & cheese into crocks, ready-to-bake.
4:00 PMFinal mise: all steak portions tempered. Grill lit + seasoned. Cold station stocked. Oysters received + inspected.
5:00 PMPre-service huddle: chef reviews 86 list, specials, VIP notes, allergy flags. Stations confirmed ready.
5:30 PMDoors open. First reservations 5:45 PM.
Service Scripts
🥩 Steak Temperature Verification
"Before I place your order — just to make sure we get your steak exactly right — are you preferring rare, medium-rare, medium, or beyond that? Chef likes to confirm, especially on the porterhouse."
✓ Confirm at order · Re-confirm at delivery. "Medium-rare for you — beautiful colour, warm red centre. Enjoy."
🍷 Wine Upsell Script
"The porterhouse is a serious piece of beef — it really sings with a bold Napa Cab. We have a Jordan Cab that's a house favourite, or if you're looking for something special, the Stag's Leap S.L.V. is extraordinary with dry-aged beef."
✓ Always suggest bottle first. Offer magnum for table of 4+. Never say "cheap" — say "approachable."
🍮 Dessert Close Script
"You've had a great meal — let me tempt you with our Bananas Foster. Our server does it right at the table — the whole room notices. And honestly, it's the best way to end a night like this."
✓ Lead with the experience, not the dish. Mention the flambé as a reason to say yes. Offer to split.

👥 Staff Temperature Cadence

  • On order: Server reads back temp; expo marks ticket
  • On runner delivery: Confirm guest's name and steak temp aloud
  • Two-minute check: "How is the temperature on your steak?" — not optional
  • If wrong: Pull immediately; no argument. Chef priority remake. Comp one side dish.
  • Manager visit: 100% table visits during steak course for parties of 2+

🎯 Upsell Ladder (per table)

  • Cocktail: Premium spirit → $4–8 upgrade
  • Wine: Glass → Bottle → Magnum
  • Steak: NY Strip → Ribeye → Porterhouse (for 2)
  • Add-ons: Truffle butter (+$6), lobster (+$28)
  • Sides: 2 minimum suggested per table
  • Dessert: Bananas Foster (show) → cheesecake (easy)
  • Target uplift: +$28–45 per person from upsell

Retention, Staffing & P&L

❤️ Customer Retention System

  • VIP Program: Track regulars in POS — birthday, anniversary, favourite cut, wine preferences, dietary needs. Use by name on arrival.
  • Post-Visit: Personalised email 48 hrs after first visit. Invite back with "special tasting" event — dry-age reveal dinners quarterly.
  • Birthday Protocol: Complimentary dessert + card signed by chef. Tag in reservation system 30 days out; brief staff night-of.
  • Corporate Accounts: Preferred booking for regular companies. Direct billing, reserved tables, sommelier on call. Target: 15–20% of revenue from corp accounts.
  • Loyalty Milestone: 5th visit → complimentary dry-aged tasting flight with chef. 10th visit → named on the "Wall of Regulars."
  • Feedback Loop: Manager calls top 10 guests monthly. 48-hr complaint resolution. Never let a bad experience go unanswered.
