Method — The Ancient Formula

1
The formula: "One of sour, two of sweet, three of strong, four of weak" — a centuries-old Caribbean rum punch rhyme that is a precise ratio: 1 part lime juice, 2 parts cane sugar syrup, 3 parts rum, 4 parts water or ice. The formula is universal across the Caribbean with regional variations. Trinidad: Angostura rum + Angostura bitters finish + freshly grated nutmeg. Jamaica: Appleton rum, no bitters. Barbados: Mount Gay, nutmeg garnish. This guide uses the Trinidadian version.
2
Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice. Shake vigorously 15 seconds. Strain over fresh ice. Grate fresh nutmeg over the surface — a microplane releases the volatile aromatic oils that pre-ground nutmeg completely lacks. Float two dashes of Angostura bitters — they sit on the surface in a dark, aromatic layer. The visual: gold punch, dark bitters, fresh nutmeg. Classic Trinidad.
💡 The Angostura bitters story: Invented in 1824 by a German doctor treating tropical stomach ailments in Simón Bolívar's army. The formula is a trade secret known to a handful of people. The bottle has been intentionally oversized for the label since 1850 — the founding brothers couldn't agree who made the error and kept it as a brand identifier. Every bottle of Angostura bitters in the world is made in Trinidad. No other cocktail story in any category matches this.
Cold, sweet, sour, rum-warm in the chest. The cane sugar's slight molasses complexity. The lime acidity bright and fresh. The Angostura bitters: herbal, slightly medicinal in the best way. Fresh nutmeg aromatic on the nose before every sip. This is the drink of the Caribbean — every island version is correct; this is simply the Trinidadian one.

Method

1
El Dorado and Demerara rum: El Dorado is produced at Demerara Distillers in Guyana, on the banks of the Demerara River. It uses colonial-era wooden pot stills dating to the 17th and 18th centuries — some of the last functioning stills of this type in the world. The Demerara cane grows in the region's unique black clay soil. The result: a rum of extraordinary richness — chocolatey, dried fruit, long warm finish. Used instead of Gosling's Black Seal, El Dorado 12 Year elevates this cocktail completely. The wooden-stills-from-the-1600s story is your best bar story.
2
House ginger beer (batch 2L): Combine grated fresh ginger (200g), cane sugar (150g), lime juice (2 limes), water (1.5L), dry yeast (3g). Mix. Ferment at room temperature 24–36 hours. Natural yeast produces gentle carbonation and a raw ginger intensity commercial ginger beer cannot match. Strain. Refrigerate. Shelf life 3–4 days. Rolling batch — always have a new batch fermenting.
El Dorado rises through the house ginger beer rather than hiding beneath it. The ginger beer: intensely gingery, naturally carbonated, slightly complex. The lime brightening. The crystallised ginger garnish intensifying. This is a Dark & Stormy that makes bar regulars stop mid-sip and ask what rum you used.
