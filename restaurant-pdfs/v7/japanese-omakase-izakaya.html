Concept · Philosophy · Standards
Concept DNA

A 40-seat NYC hybrid blending omakase reverence with izakaya warmth. The front room anchors a 12-seat hinoki sushi bar for counter omakase progression; the back izakaya section runs à-la-carte with shared plates and robata. One kitchen. Two dining languages. Zero compromise on ingredient quality.

Signature Moments
  • ✦ Uni nigiri — tableside assembly
  • ✦ Wagyu sukiyaki — tableside pour
  • ✦ Matcha tiramisu — finishing cart
  • ✦ Sake flight pairing — guided by chef
  • ✦ Binchōtan yakitori — live charcoal bar

Culinary Philosophy
Shokunin Spirit

Every motion purposeful. Rice seasoned to body temperature. Fish aged precisely 24–72h for peak umami. No shortcuts, no substitutes.

Izakaya Heart

The izakaya floor is alive — convivial, noisy, generous. Dishes arrive as ready. Drinks flow. Binchōtan smoke is part of the atmosphere.

Zero-Waste Kitchen

Fish trim → dashi. Vegetable ends → pickle brine. Sake lees → dessert component. Every gram has a purpose.

Binchōtan Yakitori Signature
備長炭 · 焼き鳥
Grill 650°F+ FC 22% 12 min
IngredientQty (4 skewers)Unit CostTotal
Chicken thigh, boneless280g$0.38/oz$3.76
Negi (Japanese leek)80g$0.12/oz$0.34
Tare (house soy-mirin glaze)40ml$0.09/ml$0.36
Shichimi togarashi2g$0.04/g$0.08
Binchōtan charcoal (per service)allocated—$0.80
  1. Thread chicken thigh pieces (30g each) alternating with 3cm negi sections onto bamboo skewers soaked 30 min.
  2. Light binchōtan in chimney starter; allow 25 min until glowing white — no flames.
  3. Grill skewers 3 cm above coal. Turn every 90 sec for 6 min until caramelized.
  4. Dip in tare, return to grill 1 min each side to set glaze. Repeat dip once more.
  5. Finish with generous shichimi. Serve immediately — no holding.

Omakase Mains — おまかせ
5 Nigiri / Sashimi Courses · Counter Service
Omakase Counter Protocol

Each course is served directly by the itamae (sushi chef). Rice is pressed tableside. No soy on nigiri — seasoning is applied by chef with brush. Pacing: 8–12 min between courses. Maximum 12 guests per counter seating.

  1. Rice temperature check: must be 35–40°C. Mold nigiri (25g each) in left palm with 3 precise motions.
  2. Apply microspeck wasabi directly to rice — no side-serving at counter.
