Why Hong Kong Café? HK-style cafés (Cha Chaan Teng) are among the most profitable café formats globally — high table turnover, beloved comfort food, loyal customer base. In US cities with Asian communities, demand is consistently strong. This guide gives you everything to launch, staff, and run one profitably from day one.

4Staff Forecasting

5Kitchen Workflow & Layout

🔥 Hot Section

  • 2× commercial wok burners (min 100,000 BTU)
  • 1× flat top grill for pork chops/toast
  • 1× deep fryer (spring rolls, tofu, toast)
  • 1× rice cooker station (×3 commercial)
  • 1× bain marie for congee/soups

❄️ Cold & Prep Section

  • 1× prep bench (stainless, min 2.4m)
  • 1× under-counter fridge (proteins)
  • Sago/dessert prep area (separate)
  • Dedicated noodle boiling station
  • Pass window with heat lamp

⚡ Ticket Time Targets (HK Café Standard)

  • Entrées: 4–6 minutes — pre-prepped, quick fry/steam
  • Noodle soups: 5–7 minutes — broth always on, noodles to order
  • Rice plates: 6–8 minutes — proteins pre-cooked, rice pre-steamed
  • Clay pot rice: 12–15 minutes — inform guests upfront, worth the wait
  • Desserts: 2–3 minutes — all made ahead, just plate and serve

💰 Profit Formula: Revenue ($4,800) − Food Cost ($1,440) − Labour ($1,050) − Overhead ($1,100) = Net Profit $1,210/day = ~$36,000/month. Achieve this by maintaining food cost below 30% and keeping your prime cost (food + labour) under 55%.
📈 Top 3 Profit Maximisers for HK Café:

1. Milk tea & beverages — food cost 8–12%, must push on every table. Target 1.5 drinks per cover.
2. Egg tarts & pineapple buns — bake your own, food cost under 22%, sell as add-ons at every meal.
3. Congee & macaroni soup — breakfast heroes. Food cost 18–22%, fast ticket, table turns in 25 min.
