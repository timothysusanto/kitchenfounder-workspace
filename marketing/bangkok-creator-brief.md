## Equipment & Apps Required

### Phone Camera Settings:
- Resolution: **4K at 30fps** (standard content)
- Action/movement shots: **1080p at 60fps** (smoother motion)
- Always film **vertical 9:16** — never horizontal
- Clean your lens before every shoot
- Use natural light when possible — Bangkok afternoon light (3-5pm) is best
- Avoid fluorescent overhead lighting — it looks green on camera

### Apps to Install:
1. **CapCut** — main video editor (free)
2. **Higgsfield** (higgsfield.ai) — AI video for cinematic b-roll when needed
3. **Kling** (klingai.com) — AI food/kitchen video generation
4. **InShot** — quick mobile edits (Pro $14.99/year — Mothy will provide login)
5. **Google Drive** — file delivery to Mothy
6. **Telegram** — communication with Mothy

### Google Drive Setup:
Mothy will share a folder with this structure:
```
KitchenFounder Content/
├── RAW footage/

├── Approved — ready to post/
├── Posted/
└── Assets (logos, fonts, brand kit)/
```
Always upload finished videos to "Finished videos — for approval" folder.

## Daily Workflow

### Morning (9–10am Bangkok time):
- Check content calendar for today's content type
- Review any feedback from Mothy on yesterday's content
- Plan shoot location and setup

### Afternoon (1–5pm):
- Film content — aim for 3-5 raw clips per day

- Film more than you need — you can always cut, can't add

### Evening (6–9pm):
- Edit in CapCut — add captions, music, CTA, branding
- Export and upload to Google Drive "Finished — for approval" folder
- Send Telegram message to Mothy: "2 videos uploaded for approval"

### Mothy reviews within 24 hours:
- Approved → moves to "Approved — ready to post" folder

### Script 6 — "How to Price a Dish"

It's math, not guesswork. Then check: does $20 feel right for this dish in your market? If yes, done. If it feels too high, you need to reduce the ingredient cost, not the price."*

### What to do if a video underperforms (under 200 views):
1. Check the hook — did it grab attention in 3 seconds?
2. Check the length — was it too long?
3. Check posting time — was it posted at the right time for US audience?
4. Note what didn't work, don't repeat it
5. Do NOT delete the video — it can still get picked up by the algorithm later

### What to do if a video goes viral (over 10,000 views):
1. Immediately notify Mothy via Telegram
2. Identify exactly what made it work (hook, topic, format, length)
3. Make 3 more videos on the same topic/format within 48 hours
4. Reply to every comment within the first 24 hours — this boosts reach
5. Pin the video to profile if possible
