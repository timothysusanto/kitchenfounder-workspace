## SHOPIFY DESCRIPTION

In 1977, someone in Harajuku rolled a crêpe into a cone. It seems like a small decision. It was not. The cone made the crêpe walkable, holdable, and photographable. It turned a seated French dessert into one of the most replicated street food formats in Asia. Marion Crêpes started it. Tokyo perfected it. Now it's everywhere.

This guide teaches you how to build and run the definitive version of that business — from a 3-sqm window to a franchise operation.
