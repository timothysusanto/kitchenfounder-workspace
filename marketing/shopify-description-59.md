## SHOPIFY DESCRIPTION

Daifuku. Mochi Ice Cream. Warabi Mochi. Mitarashi Dango. Sakura Mochi. Japan's most culturally embedded confection — made from glutinous rice pounded to silk, wrapped around flavours as ancient as the country itself.

This guide covers every commercial mochi format: 5 production styles, 28 full recipes, the complete seasonal mochi calendar, in-house an paste mastery, and the business model behind the specialty mochi shop format that is expanding globally.

**28 recipes across 5 commercial formats:**
- **Daifuku** — Ichigo (Strawberry), Classic Koshian, Matcha Double, Black Sesame, Yuzu Citrus, Kusa Mochi (Yomogi), Lychee Rose, Mango Coconut, Kuromitsu Kinako Cream
- **Mochi Ice Cream** — Matcha, Black Sesame, Hojicha (with glucose syrup pliability science)
- **Warabi Mochi** — Traditional translucent jelly, kinako + kuromitsu service
- **Dango** — Mitarashi (sweet soy glaze), Hanami tricolour, Yomogi
- **Sakura & Specialty** — Kanto-style Sakura Mochi, Kusa Mochi, Oshiruko red bean soup
- **Dessert formats** — The Mochi Gift Box (6-piece), Matcha Mochi Parfait, premium layered presentations

**Master fillings made in-house:** Full koshian (smooth red bean — shibukiri method), tsubu-an (chunky), shiro-an (white bean canvas for flavoured variants), matcha-an, yuzu-an, black sesame paste, kuromitsu kinako cream. Your fillings are your IP — commercial paste is the shortcut that loses you the repeat customer.

**The science your competitors don't know:** Why shiratamako vs mochiko vs joshinko vs warabiko are not interchangeable. Why glucose syrup in mochi ice cream is the only thing that keeps the skin pliable at -18°C. Why fresh daifuku must never be frozen (the skin crystallises irreversibly). The water ratio rule (1:0.85–0.90 by weight). The shibukiri method (first-boil discard) that separates professional koshian from bitter home attempts.
