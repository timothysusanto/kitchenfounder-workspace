## SHOPIFY DESCRIPTION

In 1903, Antoine Rumpelmayer piped a mound of chestnut cream over meringue at Angelina Paris and called it Mont Blanc — after the highest peak in the Alps. For a century, nothing changed. Then Japan got hold of it.

By 2023, the mont blanc had become the most photographed dessert in Japan. Beurre & Sucre in Nakameguro had queues forming at 7am for an 11am opening — not for a croissant, not for ramen. For 90 seconds of tableside chestnut cream piped from a gun into a mound nine centimetres tall. The theatre of it. The ritual of it. The Instagram of it.

This guide teaches you how to build and run that café.
