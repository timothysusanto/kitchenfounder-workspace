TIKTOK & INSTAGRAM PLAYBOOK
4 formats · 7-day posting schedule · Caption formula · Hashtag sets

📚 Format 1 — "Did You Know?" (Education)

Hook: Surprising fact about a cuisine or restaurant business
Body: 3 quick facts, each more surprising
CTA: "Save this if you're planning to open a restaurant"
Best for: Reels, TikTok, carousel

Example hooks:

  • "60% of restaurants fail in 5 years. The ones that survive all have this one thing."
  • "Filipino food is about to have its moment in America — first movers will win."
  • "The hopper is Sri Lanka's most iconic dish and almost no US restaurant is doing it."

🎬 Format 2 — "POV: You're Opening a Restaurant" (Aspirational)

Hook: Put the viewer inside the dream
Body: Walk through a day in the restaurant
CTA: "We built the full operations guide — link in bio"
Best for: TikTok, Reels

Example — Yemeni Mandi:

"POV: You're opening a Yemeni mandi restaurant in Houston. 6am. You're lowering a whole lamb into a tandoor pit. 8 hours later, you lift the lid. The rice underneath has absorbed every drop. This is mandi. TikTok has 50 million views on mandi videos. Almost no US restaurant is doing it right."

🏆 Format 3 — "The Secret Behind [Famous Restaurant]" (Authority)

Hook: Name-drop a famous restaurant and reveal something interesting
Best for: Instagram carousel, long-form TikTok

  • "The secret behind Dishoom London's cult status (and how to replicate it in the US)"
  • "Why Hoppers London has a 2-hour wait — and what that tells you about the US market"
  • "What Nobu taught the world about Peruvian-Japanese food (and the gap it left)"

🔄 Format 4 — "Before vs After" (Transformation)

Hook: Show the pain vs the solution

Before KitchenFounder:
❌ No food cost system — losing money on every plate
❌ No service scripts — staff winging it every night
❌ No P&L model — no idea if you'll survive year 1

After KitchenFounder:
✅ Every recipe has exact food cost % calculated
✅ Word-for-word scripts for every moment of service
✅ Full P&L showing exactly what you need to break even
$29.99. Less than a dinner out.
