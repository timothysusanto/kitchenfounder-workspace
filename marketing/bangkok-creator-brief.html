About KitchenFounder

KitchenFounder sells restaurant operations guides to first-time restaurant owners in the US market. Each guide has recipes, food cost models, service training, staff workflows, and P&L projections — everything a chef needs to know before opening.

Products: Restaurant PDF Guides ($29.99 each) · Food Cost Calculator App ($19.99) · Free Restaurant Opening Checklist
Platforms: @kitchenfounder on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest
Store: kitchen-founder.myshopify.com · kitchenfounder.etsy.com
Target audience: 25–35 year olds in the US who want to open their first restaurant — passionate, have some savings, leaving 9-5.

Brand Voice

Professional but human. Chef authority. Educational without being academic. Think: your most knowledgeable chef friend — not a textbook. Never corporate. Never boring. Always real kitchen experience.

Daily Workflow

AM
Morning (9–10am Bangkok time) — Check content calendar for today's content type. Review any feedback from Mothy on yesterday's content. Plan shoot location and setup.
PM
Afternoon (1–5pm) — Film content. Aim for 3–5 raw clips per day. Bangkok afternoon light is best from 3–5pm. Film more than you need — you can always cut, you can't add.
🌙
Evening (6–9pm) — Edit in CapCut: add captions, music, CTA, KitchenFounder watermark. Export and upload to Google Drive "Finished — for approval" folder. Send Telegram to Mothy: "2 videos uploaded for approval."
✅
Mothy reviews within 24 hours — Approved → moves to "Approved — ready to post." Changes requested → specific feedback given. Re-edit and re-upload.

SCRIPT 02
"Why 90% of Restaurants Fail"
🪝 Hook (0–3 sec)
"90% of restaurants fail in the first year. Here's the real reason — and it's not what you think."
💡 Value (3–45 sec)

Menu priced by feeling, not by formula. I've seen it happen over and over in 10 years of professional kitchens. The food is great. The passion is real. But the business side kills it. That's why I built KitchenFounder — so the passionate people actually make it."
📣 CTA (last 3 sec)

SCRIPT 04
"The One Number Every Chef Must Know"
🪝 Hook (0–3 sec)
"There is one number that determines if your restaurant lives or dies. Most chefs don't know it."
💡 Value (3–40 sec)
"It's your food cost percentage. Ingredient cost divided by selling price, times 100. If your chicken dish costs $4 to make and you sell it for $20, your food cost is 20%. That's excellent. If it costs $8 and you sell it for $20, that's 40%. You're losing money before labour, rent, and utilities. Every dish on your menu should sit between 22 and 32 percent. Go above that consistently and your restaurant will fail no matter how full it is."
📣 CTA (last 3 sec)
"Free food cost calculator in bio — works in any browser, no install needed."
SCRIPT 05
Thai Street Food Technique — Wok Hei
🪝 Hook (0–3 sec)
[Wok over high flame] "This is why Thai food tastes different when you cook it at home."
💡 Value (3–45 sec)
"Wok hei. The smoky, slightly charred flavour from a Thai street food stall. You can't replicate it on a home stove — a street vendor's wok burner runs at 50,000 BTU. Your home stove runs at 10,000. The solution for a restaurant kitchen: cast iron wok, burner on maximum, small batches, fast movement. Never overcrowd the wok. One portion at a time. The moment you add too much food, the temperature drops and you're steaming, not frying."
📣 CTA (last 3 sec)
"Full Thai Street Food guide in bio — 28 recipes with exact techniques."
SCRIPT 06
"How to Price a Dish"
🪝 Hook (0–3 sec)
"Stop pricing your menu by what feels right. Here's the formula."
💡 Value (3–40 sec)
"Step one: calculate your ingredient cost exactly — weigh everything, cost every gram. Step two: decide your target food cost percentage (25–30% for most casual concepts). Step three: divide ingredient cost by your target percentage. That's your minimum sell price. If your dish costs $5 to make and you want 25% food cost, your sell price is $20. It's math, not guesswork. Then check: does $20 feel right for this dish in your market? If yes, done."
📣 CTA (last 3 sec)
"Food cost calculator in bio — free download, works instantly."

📉 If a video underperforms (<200 views)

  • Check the hook — did it grab attention in 3 sec?
  • Check the length — was it too long?
  • Check posting time — right time for US audience?
  • Note what didn't work. Don't repeat it.
  • Do NOT delete — algorithm can still pick it up later

🚀 If a video goes viral (>10,000 views)

  • Immediately notify Mothy via Telegram
  • Identify exactly what made it work
  • Make 3 more on same topic/format within 48 hours
  • Reply to every comment within first 24 hours
  • Pin the video to profile if possible
